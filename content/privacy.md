LoveKorea (`lovekorea.me`) publishes Korea travel, food, beauty, shopping, culture, and language guides. This policy explains the information involved when you use the site and the choices available to you.

## Information Used by the Site

You do not need a LoveKorea account to read the site. Information processed through the site and its service providers can include:

- Usage information, such as pages viewed, referring pages, device and browser information, approximate location, and interactions with links.
- Technical information needed to deliver pages, such as an IP address and browser request details processed by hosting and other service providers.
- Your email address and the information you choose to include when you contact us.
- Public comments and GitHub profile information when you use the comment system.

We use this information to understand readership, improve guides and navigation, respond to questions and corrections, and operate the website.

## Google Analytics

LoveKorea currently uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies. Interaction data can include the page and destination of marked recommendation links. This information helps us understand which recommendations readers use.

See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information about Google's handling of data.

You can manage cookies through your browser or use the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) where supported.

## Advertising and Google AdSense

Google AdSense is not yet approved, and Google ads are not currently displayed. The following explains how advertising data may be used when Google ads are displayed.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a person's previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to show ads based on visits to LoveKorea and other sites on the internet.

You can manage personalized advertising through [Google's ad settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also visit [YourAdChoices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) to learn about choices offered by participating advertising companies. Turning off personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads.

Google describes its advertising technologies and choices in [How Google uses cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) and [Advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). We will update this policy as advertising providers and site controls change.

## Affiliate and Other External Links

LoveKorea links to external websites, including travel operators, booking platforms, retailers, and reference sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may receive information when you visit them.

No affiliate program is active as of the update date above. If affiliate links are added, the relevant provider may use cookies or referral parameters to associate a purchase with a recommendation from LoveKorea. The site may receive a commission from an eligible purchase or booking. See the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for more information.

## Comments

Article comments use giscus, which connects to GitHub Discussions. Posting a comment requires a GitHub account. Comments and associated public profile details can be visible on the site and on GitHub. When you sign in through giscus, it uses browser local storage to keep you signed in.

Read the [giscus Privacy Policy](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details. Do not post private or sensitive information in public comments.

## Browser Storage and Your Choices

The site and its providers can use cookies or local storage for analytics, commenting, and site preferences. Advertising and affiliate providers may also use cookies when those features become active.

You can clear cookies and local storage, restrict them in your browser, use the Google choices linked above, and choose whether to post comments. Blocking storage or third-party services can affect sign-in, comments, or saved preferences.

## Contact Messages and Retention

If you email us, your address and message are available to the site operator and the services used to handle that email. They are used to respond to your request and related follow-up. Please send only information needed for the request.

Analytics and other service providers retain information according to the applicable service settings and their policies. For questions about information connected to a message or comment, or to request correction or deletion, contact us using the address below. Information held by a third-party service may also need to be managed through that provider.

## Contact

For privacy questions and requests, email [admin@yoonisoft.com](mailto:admin@yoonisoft.com). Include the relevant page address and a description of your request; do not send passwords or payment details.

You can also visit the [Contact page](/contact/).

## Changes to This Policy

We will revise this page when the site's analytics, advertising, affiliate links, or other data practices change. The date above identifies the latest update.
