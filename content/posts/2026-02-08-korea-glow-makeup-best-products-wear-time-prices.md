**If a dewy base turns patchy, another glow product may add to the problem.** First record where the makeup changes and what is underneath it. A controlled comparison using the products you already own is more informative than buying a primer, mist, highlighter and new cushion together.

This is a makeup comparison method, not a claim that LoveKorea has tested these products. Manufacturer information was checked on October 3, 2026.

## Separate the result you want from the problem you see

| What you notice | What to compare on a different ordinary day | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Too much shine in photographs | Less highlighter or a smaller application area | Whether the unwanted reflection is from the base or added highlight |
| Visible build-up around the nose | A thinner base application with the same preparation | Whether the same area still looks uneven |
| Product rolling into little pieces | One fewer optional layer, keeping sunscreen use unchanged | Which combination was present when it happened |
| Transfer onto clothing | The same base with versus without its recommended setting step | Transfer and comfort, rather than an assumed number of hours |
| Dry or uncomfortable skin | Stop experimenting if irritation occurs | Whether this needs skincare or professional advice rather than more makeup |

Change one optional makeup variable at a time. Do not reduce the amount of sunscreen below its directions to make foundation look smoother.

## A brand name is not a formula

MAC lists several separate products in its [Fix+ range](https://www.maccosmetics.com/collections/setting-spray), including Fix+, Fix+ Matte and Fix+ Stay Over. A review of one is not evidence about another. Compare the complete name and current package instructions before buying a replacement.

The [Fix+ Stay Over product page](https://www.maccosmetics.com/products/fix-stay-over-24hr-setting-spray?size=100ml) advertises 24-hour wear. That is a manufacturer claim, not our independent wear result or a promise for a day of sightseeing in Seoul. Its instructions specify closed eyes and mouth and a spraying distance of 10–12 inches. Use the directions for the exact version you own.

No mist can be assumed to make every base waterproof. Check the particular claim and its conditions rather than transferring a claim from another item in the range.

## Keep a short wear log

Use the same lighting for comparison photographs where practical. Save these fields in your phone:

- Date and the type of day: mostly indoors, walking outside, or attending an event.
- Skin preparation and sunscreen used as directed.
- Exact base, shade and optional primer or setting product.
- Application amount in terms you can repeat, such as one light layer.
- Observations after application and later in the day: shine, transfer, separation, comfort and any touch-ups.

The purpose is to find what changed in your own routine. It does not turn one day's experience into a clinical study or establish that a product will last the same time for other people.

If you have a major event, test the routine on a normal day first. A package claiming long wear is not a reason to introduce several unfamiliar formulas immediately before the event.

## Choose one purchase that answers the log

If the base shade is wrong, another setting spray will not correct that mismatch. If only a small area needs a touch-up, consider whether the compact or concealer you already own can do it. If you want more reflected light on the cheekbones, compare a sample of one highlight rather than rebuilding the whole routine.

For cost comparison, use actual shelf prices and the exact size. A hypothetical ₩18,000 bottle of 30 ml is ₩600/ml; a ₩36,000 bottle of 100 ml is ₩360/ml. The bigger bottle has a lower unit price but doubles the purchase cost. A first-time trial may justify the smaller amount. These figures are examples, not MAC prices or Korean market averages.

## Stop treating discomfort as a makeup problem

Persistent stinging, swelling or a rash calls for stopping the new cosmetic and getting appropriate medical advice. Follow the [AAD's guidance for testing new skincare and cosmetics](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/prevent-skin-problems/test-skin-care-products/) before widespread use. A “glow” finish is not evidence of improved skin health.
