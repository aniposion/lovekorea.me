**Do not send one friend with one passport to buy playoff tickets for the whole group.** KBO's October 20, 2026 notice allows foreign passport holders to buy **one ticket per person** at the stadium on game day. Plan for each traveler to attend with their own passport. This is a useful route when online booking is blocked, but it does not guarantee a seat. [Read KBO's playoff ticket notice](https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12210).

**Scope: 2026 postseason rules checked October 24, 2026.** Check the notice for your exact series and stadium before traveling; this guide does not confirm remaining inventory or next season's policy.

## Online sold out: which queue are you asking about?

There are two different questions to ask at the ground. “Are there any tickets left?” may send you to the general sales line. A foreign visitor should also ask whether the **dedicated digital-access ticket window (디지털 취약계층)** is operating for that game.

| Route | What the current official information says |
|---|---|
| Dedicated access window | NOL lists a **200-ticket limit shared by visitors aged 65+, disabled visitors and foreigners**. Purchases use the dedicated window. |
| General game-day sales | Remaining seats only; sold-out means no purchase through this route. |
| Online booking | KBO names NOL as the exclusive postseason seller, with a maximum of four tickets per person. That online limit does not change the passport window's one-person limit. |

Sources: [NOL's current postseason guide](https://nol.yanolja.com/ticket/genre/sports/postseason) and [KBO's October 20 notice](https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12210). The shared allocation can run out. No official source here promises that arriving at a particular time will secure tickets.

## Make a group decision before joining the line

**Example: three friends, one person sent ahead.** If that person plans to use the passport route, the one-person limit will not cover three seats. Arrange for everyone who needs to buy to bring their passport and attend; confirm the stadium's procedure before splitting up.

Agree on these points beforehand:

- **Separate seats:** is the group willing to sit apart if adjacent seats are unavailable?
- **A partial result:** what will you do if only some people can buy? Do not assume an issued ticket can be handed to another person.
- **The time budget:** set a point at which you leave the queue and switch to another plan.
- **The journey home:** check the return service before committing to an intercity trip for an unconfirmed seat.

A passport establishes eligibility for the visitor route. It does not reserve inventory for everyone traveling with you.

## Check the opening time and the actual window

NOL lists general box-office operation from **two hours before first pitch until the end of the eighth inning**. KBO also says cancelled tickets, if available, may be sold from two hours before the game. An opening time is not an estimate of when a queue begins.

Ask staff where the dedicated window is **at your stadium**. The current NOL page specifies a location for Gwangju and Jamsil; that does not identify the Daegu window. Avoid following a Jamsil queue map for a game in another city.

Useful questions to show staff:

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 외국인 현장 구매 전용 창구가 어디인가요? | Where is the dedicated window for foreigners buying on site? |
| 이 창구는 몇 시에 열어요? | What time does this window open? |
| 전용 좌석이 아직 남아 있나요? | Are any seats in the dedicated allocation left? |
| 일행과 붙어 앉을 수 있나요? | Can we sit together? |

Save the stadium's Korean name and address before leaving. Our [Naver Map address guide](/posts/naver-map-hotel-address-search/) explains how to resolve English-name search problems and check the pin.

## Regular-season advice can send you to the wrong place

For a regular-season game, start with the home club's own ticket policy. For example, Kiwoom's [March 20, 2026 announcement](https://heroesbaseball.co.kr/mobile/story/heroesNews/view.do?num=22460) included foreign visitors in a shared 200-seat access scheme at Gocheok: 100 burgundy seats and 100 outfield seats. Its [ticket page](https://heroesbaseball.co.kr/ticket/normal/viewCharge.do) identifies a dedicated window at the third-base infield box office.

Those details describe **Kiwoom's 2026 home-game policy**. They do not establish a postseason window location, current availability or a 2027 entitlement. Match any advice you find to the home team, season and competition stage.

## If you already have an online ticket

Follow its admission instructions. [NOL says postseason mobile entry](https://nol.yanolja.com/ticket/genre/sports/postseason) uses the live ticket in the app; **a screenshot or ticket image is not accepted**. Check that you can open the required ticket before leaving Wi-Fi. At an in-person purchase, ask staff how that ticket is issued and used.

**Before traveling:** confirm the game and venue, bring each buyer's passport, ask about the dedicated allocation, and keep a backup evening plan. The useful advantage is knowing which official route to ask for and what its limit means for your group.
