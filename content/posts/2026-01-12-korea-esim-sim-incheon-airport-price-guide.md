# Korea eSIM or Airport SIM in 2026: Incheon Pickup, Prices & Setup

**Checked September 24, 2026.** Arriving at Incheon Airport, you can either install a Korea travel **eSIM** on a compatible unlocked phone or collect a **physical SIM** at an airport telecom counter. An eSIM avoids a SIM-card swap; a physical SIM works for travelers whose phones have a compatible tray but no usable eSIM. Either way, buy a plan for **Korea**, your actual travel dates and the calls or texts you need.

The old “eSIM always costs less” rule is unreliable. On the same LG U+ tourist product pages checked today, a **five-day data eSIM and five-day data SIM are each listed at ₩27,500**. The carrier also lists a one-day data eSIM at **₩6,500**. Those are examples from **one provider**, not a guarantee that every plan or checkout costs the same. See the [LG U+ eSIM](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/product/esim?tab=data) and [SIM card](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/product/simcard?tab=data) pages for current options before you pay.

## Choose by phone and service, then compare the price

| What you need | Start with | Check before purchase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maps, messaging and web data on an unlocked eSIM-capable phone | Korea data eSIM | Device and carrier compatibility, QR installation, validity start and refund rule |
| Data on a phone without a usable eSIM, but with a SIM tray | Physical Korea data SIM | SIM size, unlocked status, airport pickup counter and its hours |
| Korean outgoing calls or texts | A product that explicitly includes voice/SMS | Whether passport verification at the airport is required; any extra call balance |
| Banking or government identity verification in Korea | Ask the relevant service what it accepts | A short-stay tourist number may not qualify |

Do not buy a **data-only** product because it shows a Korean phone number if you need to send a text. LG U+'s [product FAQ](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/support/faq?tab=esim) says its data-only eSIM cannot send calls or texts and cannot be upgraded with a voice top-up later. Its data-plus-voice option requires a passport check at an airport counter for voice service. The provider also says that even voice/SMS service does **not** provide banking or government identity verification. Rules at other operators can differ, so check the exact product.

Refunds matter more than a small price gap. The [LG U+ data eSIM page](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/product/esim?tab=data) says to activate within **60 days of purchase**, that the eSIM cannot be reissued after installation, and that completed data eSIM purchases cannot be cancelled or refunded. These are **LG U+ product terms**, not universal rules for all Korea eSIMs. If your trip may change, compare the seller's terms before payment.

## Check your phone before buying

1. **Carrier lock:** Ask your home carrier whether the device is unlocked. On iPhone, Apple's [travel eSIM guide](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/118227) says to check Settings → General → About → Network Provider Lock; “No SIM Restrictions” means it is unlocked.
2. **eSIM hardware and region:** LG U+ suggests dialing **\*#06#** and looking for an **EID**, but also lists provider-specific device exclusions. A model name alone is not enough. Samsung's [compatibility guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/galaxy-esim-and-supported-network-carriers/) warns that eSIM support can vary by country of origin. Check your exact model with the **seller** before buying.
3. **Physical tray:** If choosing a physical SIM, check that your phone actually has a SIM tray and accepts another carrier's card. Some newer phones are eSIM-only in some markets.
4. **Home-number needs:** If you need bank codes sent to your existing number, check whether your phone can keep the home line active for calls/texts while using the Korea line for data. Check your home carrier's roaming charges before leaving that line on.

An eSIM needs an internet connection to download. The LG U+ page shows QR installation under **Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM** on iPhone, or **Settings → Connections → SIM Manager → Add eSIM** on supported Samsung phones. Follow your seller's instructions and keep the QR details available on a second screen or device. Do not delete an installed eSIM while troubleshooting; LG U+ says a deleted one cannot be restored.

## Where to collect a SIM at Incheon

Incheon's [official telecom counter directory](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1546/subview.do) lists KT, LG U+ and SK Telecom roaming centers in both terminals. It currently lists these **24-hour arrival-area counters**:

| Terminal | Airport directory location |
| --- | --- |
| **T1** | Near Arrival Hall **F**, west side of the first floor, for KT, LG U+ and SK Telecom |
| **T2** | Near first-floor **Exit 4** for KT and LG U+; near **Exit 7** for SK Telecom |

The airport directory identifies the desks and hours, but your **product voucher** determines where that SIM can be picked up. Confirm the correct terminal, counter, opening hours and required ID on the provider's current instructions. LG U+ says walk-in purchase is possible at its customer centers without a prior reservation, but availability and checkout terms should still be checked for your arrival.

If you arrive with an eSIM already installed and activated according to the seller's instructions, there is no physical SIM to collect. Some optional card or promotion benefits may still require a counter visit; read the selected product rather than assuming every eSIM order is fully airport-free.

## Set up the Korea line without accidental roaming

- **Before flying:** Save your order, QR code and support contact. Read when the validity clock starts; do not assume installation and first data use mean the same thing for every provider.
- **At arrival:** Connect to airport Wi-Fi if the eSIM still needs downloading. Insert a physical SIM or enable the installed eSIM following the seller's instructions.
- **In phone settings:** Select the Korea line for **mobile data**. Keep home-line data roaming off unless you intentionally purchased a roaming plan. If your Korea product requires data roaming, enable it **for that travel line only** according to its instructions.
- **Before leaving the airport:** Load a map and a web page. If service fails, confirm the selected data line, restart once, then contact the seller or visit its counter. Do not repeatedly scan or delete a one-time eSIM QR without support advice.
