**A cheaper room can cost more once you add airport transfers, meals, and repeated trips across Seoul.** For a palace-and-shopping itinerary, start your search around Myeongdong and central Seoul. For evenings in Hongdae, start near Hongik University Station. For several days at COEX, start around Samseong. Then compare the exact hotel address and your total trip cost.

This is a location-planning guide based on operator information checked **October 3, 2026**. We have not stayed at or independently inspected the hotels mentioned below. Property examples explain location choices; they are not a ranked list or a promise of room quality.

## Pick the area from your actual itinerary

| Your main plan | Start looking around | The tradeoff to check |
|---|---|---|
| Palaces, central shopping, and several short sightseeing stops | Myeongdong, Euljiro, or City Hall | A hotel marketed as “Myeongdong” may use a different station from Myeongdong Station. Check its street address. |
| Hongdae evenings and an airport rail connection | Hongik University Station | A useful airport connection does not tell you whether your room faces a noisy street. Read room-specific recent reviews. |
| COEX meetings or events on multiple days | Samseong / COEX | “Gangnam” is a large area. A hotel near Gangnam Station is not automatically convenient for COEX. |
| One early KTX departure after sightseeing | An area with an easy route to Seoul Station | Compare the final station transfer and luggage walk, using your actual departure time. |

These are our planning judgments, not measured travel-time rankings. Put your hotel and the first and last stop of each day into a journey planner. Count transfers as well as minutes. A route that is acceptable once with a day bag may be tiring twice a day with children or luggage.

## Two real examples that show why the station matters

**Hongdae: Holiday Inn Express Seoul Hongdae.** [IHG's hotel page](https://www.ihg.com/holidayinnexpress/hotels/us/en/seoul/seohg/hoteldetail) places it at Hongik University Station Exit 5; its [directions page](https://www.ihg.com/holidayinnexpress/hotels/us/en/seoul/seohg/hoteldetail/directions) gives the address as 188 Yanghwa-ro. Use that address when planning the airport-to-door route. Confirm the exact room, occupancy, and cancellation terms in your quote.

**COEX: GLAD Gangnam COEX Center.** The hotel's [official location page](https://www.glad-hotels.com/gangnamcoex/hotel/location.do?locale=en) lists 610 Teheran-ro and Samseong Station, Line 2, Exit 1. This is a useful example for a COEX-focused search. Do not substitute a different property with “Gangnam” in its name and assume the same route.

The links above go to the hotels' official pages. Compare other properties using the same checks; these two examples do not establish which hotel is cheapest or best for your dates.

## Compare the whole-stay cost

Use the final quote for the **same dates, guest ages, number of rooms, bed setup, and cancellation conditions**. If rates differ by night, divide the total room charge by the number of nights to get the nightly average for the calculator. Include all your rooms in that figure.

For extras, add only costs that you expect to pay and that are not already in the room quote: airport transfers, additional city travel, breakfast, luggage storage, or required fees. Use the same approach for both hotels. A refundable deposit is not automatically a cost; check whether it is a temporary hold or a nonrefundable charge.

**Hypothetical example, not hotel prices:** over three nights, Hotel A at ₩120,000 a night plus ₩20,000 of extras totals **₩380,000**. Hotel B at ₩110,000 a night plus ₩60,000 of extras totals **₩390,000**. The lower nightly rate costs ₩10,000 more overall. Your actual result depends on the quotes and transport choices you enter.

Cost is only one part of the decision. If the totals are close, compare the walking route with luggage, the last trip home, and refund flexibility. Do not assign a cash value to time unless you decide what that time is worth to your own group.

## Check the airport connection separately

- **Using rail:** confirm the airport terminal, rail service, transfer station, and final walk. Our [AREX Express versus All-Stop guide](/posts/incheon-airport-arex-guide/) explains why the service name matters.
- **Using an airport bus:** search the exact hotel or nearby stop, then verify the direction and departure point. See the [Incheon airport bus payment guide](/posts/incheon-airport-limousine-bus-ticket-vs-tmoney-guide/).
- **Arriving late:** compare your likely exit time after immigration and baggage with the last usable connection. Plan an alternative before choosing a nonrefundable room.

## Send these questions before paying

Copy the questions relevant to your stay into a message to the property:

> For my dates and guest count, does this room include all taxes and required fees? What are the bed sizes and maximum occupancy? Is breakfast included for every guest? Which entrance has the easiest step-free route from the station? Can you accommodate my arrival time, and what is the cancellation deadline in local time?

Ask separately about an elevator, cot, connecting room, or luggage storage if any of those determines whether the stay will work. A photograph or a general amenities icon may not apply to the room you selected.

## Before you lock in the booking

1. Save the exact room and rate conditions, not just the property's name.
2. Confirm the hotel address against your daily route and airport connection.
3. Compare final totals in one currency. If paying in another currency, check your payment provider's conversion and fees.
4. Keep the cancellation deadline and check-in instructions with your travel documents.
