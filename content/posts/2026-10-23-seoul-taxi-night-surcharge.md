**A regular Seoul taxi starts at ₩6,700 from 11 p.m. to 2 a.m., compared with ₩5,800 in the preceding hour.** That is the 40% night-surcharge band, and the listed base fare already includes it. Do not add another 40% to ₩6,700. The [Seoul government's fare table](https://news.seoul.go.kr/traffic/archives/1659?listPage=1) is the reference for these amounts.

This guide covers **Seoul's regular mid-size metered taxis**. Premium cars, large vans and taxis licensed elsewhere can use different tariffs. Official pages were checked **October 23, 2026**; these regular-taxi rates are listed as effective from February 1, 2023, rather than a new October price increase.

## The four time bands to save before an evening out

All times below are local Seoul time. Each base fare covers the **first 1.6km** under the standard tariff; the final meter can include further distance and time charges.

| Time | Night surcharge | Listed base fare |
|---|---|---|
| 4 a.m.–10 p.m. | None | ₩4,800 |
| 10–11 p.m. | 20% | ₩5,800 |
| 11 p.m.–2 a.m. | 40% | ₩6,700 |
| 2–4 a.m. | 20% | ₩5,800 |

Use the published won amounts when comparing the starting fares. Simply multiplying ₩4,800 by 1.4 gives ₩6,720, which is not the listed ₩6,700 base fare. The tariff and meter handle the actual calculation.

The [city's English taxi guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/taxi/) also lists higher distance and time increments at night. The distance increment is ₩100 per 131m during daytime, ₩120 in the 20% bands and ₩140 in the 40% band. Time can contribute when traffic is slow. The higher starting fare is only one part of the difference.

## Waiting until 2 a.m. reduces the base by ₩900

The arithmetic is **₩6,700 − ₩5,800 = ₩900**. At 2 a.m. the night surcharge falls to 20%; the daytime rate resumes at 4 a.m. Waiting for 2 a.m. does not make the whole ride ₩900 cheaper by definition, because the trip's other charges can also differ.

Before extending your evening for a lower rate, compare that possible saving with the wait, any extra spending and the availability of a suitable ride. If you are ready to leave at 1 a.m., staying out another hour solely to reduce the starting fare may be a poor trade. Use your actual trip estimate and circumstances.

For a planned late dinner or show, work backward from the venue's end time. Save the hotel address, decide where you can reasonably meet a driver, and check the last usable public-transport connection if that is your preferred return. A citywide phrase such as “the last train” does not identify your particular line, direction or transfer.

## Compare one complete quote for the whole group

The [Korean fare page](https://news.seoul.go.kr/traffic/archives/1659?listPage=1) distinguishes metered fares, approved alternative tariffs, tolls and calling charges. Check the selected vehicle and what its displayed amount includes before comparing apps or transport modes.

**Illustrative budget, not a route quote:** if a suitable car's estimated total is ₩24,000, three people sharing it would budget ₩8,000 each. If ₩24,000 already includes the night rate, adding 40% again would produce an incorrect ₩33,600 comparison. First establish what the quote covers; then divide the total among the passengers it can actually carry.

Use this short note while checking a ride:

- **Vehicle:** regular taxi, premium car or larger vehicle?
- **Journey:** exact pickup and destination, at the intended travel time?
- **Amount:** estimate, meter-based fare or confirmed total? Which extras are included?
- **Capacity:** can everyone and their bags fit in that vehicle?
- **Payment:** automatic app payment or payment to the driver?

Our [Kakao T payment guide](/posts/kakao-t-card-pay-driver/) explains the final question and what to check when card registration fails. A working payment method does not establish the fare or guarantee that a driver is available.

## Apply this to the return journey you will actually make

A night in Hongdae followed by a return to a nearby hotel is a different budget decision from crossing Seoul after a venue closes. When comparing a [Hongdae or Itaewon pub crawl](/posts/itaewon-hongdae-pub-crawl-tours-how-to-book-prices/), ask where the hosted route ends before estimating the ride home.

This table is not a fixed airport-transfer quote or a quote for a journey spanning two time bands. Airport trips need their own vehicle, toll and route checks. For a late flight arrival, the [Incheon night-bus comparison](/posts/incheon-night-bus-late-arrival/) helps you compare a reachable bus departure with the taxi total for your group.

Save the time-band table, but make the final choice from the actual pickup, vehicle and full journey. That keeps a low starting price from becoming an unrealistic return-trip budget.
