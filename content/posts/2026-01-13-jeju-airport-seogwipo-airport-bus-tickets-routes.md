# Jeju Airport to Seogwipo Bus 2026: 600 vs 800/801, Fares & Stops

**The right bus from Jeju Airport depends on your exact stop, not just “Seogwipo” in the address.** For **ICC JEJU** or Jungmun resort hotels, start with **600**; some **601** trips also serve ICC. The [ICC JEJU transport guide](https://www.iccjeju.co.kr/guide/02.php) estimates **60–70 minutes from the airport to ICC** by limousine bus. For the newer Seogwipo Bus Terminal, check **800**; for the Seogwipo Registry Office (서귀포등기소) area, check **801**. Bus **600** continues to **Seogwipo KAL Hotel**; **800 and 801 do not**. These distinctions come from the [Jeju Bus Information System's route list](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule), checked September 23, 2026.

| Your destination | First route to check | Confirm before boarding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jungmun Tourist Complex (중문관광단지), resort hotels or International Convention Center | **600**; some **601** trips end at the convention center | The exact hotel stop; 601 does not continue to the KAL Hotel |
| World Cup Stadium, Seogwipo Harbor or Seogwipo KAL Hotel (서귀포칼호텔) | **600** | Your stop on the airport-to-KAL direction |
| Seogwipo Bus Terminal (서귀포버스터미널), in the newer western part of the city | **800** | The displayed destination and your hotel's walking distance |
| Seogwipo Registry Office (서귀포등기소) area | **801** | Whether your hotel is near that stop |
| Jeju City (제주시), on the airport side of the island | Look up your *specific* hotel stop in the [official Jeju route finder](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/listSchedule) | Do not ride south to Seogwipo if your hotel is in Jeju City |

## Which route actually serves your stop?

**600: Jungmun and the southern coastal hotel corridor.** The [official 600 stop list](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/line/detailLine/405660001?alarmStationId=0&mode=normal&type=line) runs from Jeju Airport through Donggwang, Jungmun Tourist Complex and the International Convention Center, then World Cup Stadium, Seogwipo Harbor and Seogwipo KAL Hotel. A hotel described as “in Seogwipo” may still be far from that final stop. Match its Korean address to an actual stop before choosing the bus. The [Jeju Bus route summary](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) also lists **601**, but shows it terminating at the convention center rather than continuing to KAL Hotel.

![Jeju Airport bus route planning illustration](/images/jeju-airport-seogwipo-airport-bus-tickets-routes-h2-1.webp)

**800: Seogwipo Bus Terminal.** The [official 800/801 route detail](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailScheduleNew?scheduleId=2352) shows 800 passing through Jeju Airport and ending at Seogwipo Bus Terminal. It **does not go through Jungmun Tourist Complex or end at KAL Hotel**.

**801: Seogwipo Registry Office.** The same [official route detail](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailScheduleNew?scheduleId=2352) shows 801 ending at Seogwipo Transfer Stop/Registry Office (서귀포환승정류장·서귀포등기소). It shares much of 800's approach from the airport but has a different southern endpoint. If your accommodation is around central Seogwipo, check its distance from that stop; “central” alone is not a precise bus stop.

![Comparison of Jeju Airport bus options](/images/jeju-airport-seogwipo-airport-bus-tickets-routes-h2-2.webp)

**Avoid a number mix-up:** 800, 801 and the limited **800-1** are separate services. The [Jeju Bus route summary](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) describes 800-1 as an airport-to-Registry-Office service for morning or late-night periods. Confirm the number, direction and stop shown on the vehicle display before getting on.

## Jeju Airport ↔ ICC JEJU by bus 600

For **ICC JEJU 1**, the venue says to get off at **제주국제컨벤션센터/중문대포해안주상절리대** (Jeju International Convention Center/Jungmun Daepo Coast). For **ICC JEJU 2**, it says to get off at **씨에스호텔** (The Seaes Hotel). Both are on the 600/601 corridor, but they are different stops; confirm which building your event uses. The [venue's Korean directions](https://www.iccjeju.co.kr/guide/02.php) quote **₩4,500 one way for an adult from the airport to ICC** and **60–70 minutes on the bus**. These are planning figures, not a live arrival prediction; add time for baggage, the wait at the airport and walking to your event entrance.

For the **return to Jeju Airport**, wait at the stop serving the airport-bound direction. ICC JEJU identifies the return boarding area as **the rotary opposite the convention center** for its first building; verify the exact stop and next airport-bound bus on the [official route search](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/listSchedule) before leaving your event. The venue's published departure and fare details should also be rechecked on your travel date.

## Fare, payment and tickets

The [Jeju Bus fare table](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule), checked September 23, 2026, lists **adult 600 fares at ₩1,300–₩5,500** and **800-family limousine fares at ₩1,300–₩5,000**, depending on the segment. These are route-wide ranges, **not a quoted airport-to-your-hotel fare**. For the specific airport-to-ICC trip, the [ICC JEJU guide](https://www.iccjeju.co.kr/guide/02.php) quotes **₩4,500 for an adult**. Check the route-specific fare chart or ask the driver with your destination stop before paying.

**Bring a working transport card.** ICC JEJU's [current Korean directions](https://www.iccjeju.co.kr/guide/02.php) say its airport limousine is **cashless** and accepts prepaid or postpaid transport cards. A broader [Jeju tourism guide](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/themtour/view?contentsid=CNTS_200000000012534&menuId=DOM_700000000010790) also recommends a rechargeable card such as T-money, but its general cash-payment advice does not override the venue's specific instruction. Ask the driver where to tap off if needed. **Do not assume an online reservation is required** for these public airport buses. If a seller offers a “Jeju airport bus ticket,” first check which public route and stop it actually covers.

## Where to board and how to check the last departure

The [Korea Airports Corporation's Jeju Airport page](https://www.airport.co.kr/jeju/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=2&MENU_ID=100) places **600 at the limousine stop in front of Gate 5**. This is a boarding-location statement for **600**; check the airport's current signs for 800, 801 and 800-1 instead of assuming they use that exact bay.

**Verify the timetable on your travel day.** Official pages currently disagree on 600's first departure: the airport's [Korean page](https://www.airport.co.kr/jeju/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=2&MENU_ID=100) lists **06:30**, while the [Jeju Bus 600/601 timetable](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=1532), marked effective August 1, 2024, lists **06:00**. Both list a 22:20 last airport departure. The airport's English page differs again, so treat all fixed times as provisional. Check the [current Jeju Bus schedules](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/listSchedule) and the airport display before planning an early or late connection. For 800 and 801, use their [route-specific timetable](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailScheduleNew?scheduleId=2352); terminal departure times are **not** necessarily airport boarding times.

If your flight lands near the last service, leave time for baggage claim, reaching the stop and waiting for the next bus. Have a fallback plan such as an airport taxi or a night near the airport. Travel time depends on your stop and traffic; do not book a timed tour immediately after landing based on an on-bus estimate alone.

## A two-minute check before you leave the terminal

1. Save your accommodation's **Korean name and address**, then find its nearest bus stop.
2. Use the table above to choose a likely route and open the [official route and schedule search](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/listSchedule).
3. At the airport, confirm the **route number, southern destination, bay and next departure** on the display or with staff.
4. Have a working transport card ready, check your route's current payment method and tell the driver your destination stop if you need the exact fare.

### Frequently asked questions

**Does 800 or 801 go to Seogwipo KAL Hotel?** No. The [official route list](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) identifies 600 as the route continuing to KAL Hotel. 800 goes to Seogwipo Bus Terminal; 801 goes to the Registry Office area.

**Which bus goes through Jungmun?** Start with 600. Check whether a particular 601 trip is useful if you are going only as far as the International Convention Center.

**How long is bus 600 from Jeju Airport to ICC JEJU?** The [venue estimates 60–70 minutes](https://www.iccjeju.co.kr/guide/02.php) on the bus. Add airport exit, waiting and walking time. For Seogwipo KAL Hotel farther along the route, an [official Jeju tourism guide](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/themtour/view?contentsid=CNTS_200000000012534&menuId=DOM_700000000010790) gives an older estimate of about **80 minutes**; check current conditions rather than treating it as a fixed timetable.

**Can I take bus 600 from ICC JEJU back to Jeju Airport?** Yes; 600 serves both directions. Use the airport-bound stop at the [rotary opposite ICC's first building](https://www.iccjeju.co.kr/guide/02.php) and confirm the next airport-bound departure before you leave.

**Is Seogwipo Bus Terminal the same as central Seogwipo?** No. Use your hotel's Korean address to compare Seogwipo Bus Terminal with the Registry Office area and the individual 600 stops.

**How much is the bus from the airport?** The fare depends on where you get off. The official adult *route-wide* range is ₩1,300–₩5,500 for 600 and ₩1,300–₩5,000 for the 800 family; use the [official fare information](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) for your segment.
