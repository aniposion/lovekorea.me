**Take bus 600 from Jeju Airport to Jungmun, ICC JEJU or Seogwipo's coastal hotel stops: the adult fare is ₩4,500 to Jungmun/ICC and ₩5,500 to Seogwipo KAL Hotel, with about 93–103 minutes scheduled to KAL Hotel.** Choose **800** for Seogwipo Bus Terminal or **801** for the Registry Office; **601** serves Jungmun and ends at ICC JEJU.

Times are local Korean time; ride times are calculated from the official timetable's airport and destination columns, excluding waiting and walking. The 801 fare and the 800/801 boarding bays could not be verified today; check those at the airport. The linked fare workbook labels its Registry Office sheet **800-1**, so it does not establish an 801 fare.

## Which bus gets you closest to your hotel?

**Jungmun resorts or ICC JEJU:** use 600 or 601. The official route notes distinguish hotel entrances from hotel stops: 600 serves the entrances for Lotte, Shilla and Suites, while 601 serves the hotels themselves. For **ICC JEJU 1**, get off at **제주국제컨벤션센터/중문대포해안주상절리대**; for **ICC JEJU 2**, use **씨에스호텔** (The Seaes Hotel). The [ICC directions][icc] identify those separate stops.

**World Cup Stadium, Seogwipo Harbor or KAL Hotel:** use 600. Do not take 601 for these destinations: it ends at the convention center. The [600/601 route and timetable][out600] show the stops each service reaches.

**Seogwipo Bus Terminal or the Registry Office:** choose 800 or 801 respectively. Their [official stop list][out800] runs via Hoesu and Seogwipo City Hall 2, rather than the Jungmun resort hotel loop. Save your hotel's Korean address and compare its nearest stop with the route; a “Seogwipo” address alone is not enough to choose a bus.

If you are still choosing a base, compare [Jeju City, Jungmun and Seogwipo without a car](/posts/jeju-without-car-where-to-stay/) before booking accommodation.

## How do you pay for the airport bus?

**Have a loaded transport card ready.** [ICC JEJU][icc] describes its 600/601 airport limousine service as cashless, accepting prepaid or postpaid transport cards. The [Jeju Bus card guide][busguide] lists T-money among compatible prepaid cards and says cards can be purchased and recharged at convenience stores. Show the driver your destination stop before paying; the fares in the table apply to the named destinations.

The [600 fare chart][fare600] is a stop-to-stop price matrix, not a flat fare for all of Seogwipo. Read from **공항** (airport) to your destination. Likewise, the [800 fare chart][fare800] quotes the airport-to-Seogwipo-terminal journey separately from shorter trips. These are public bus fares; if a seller advertises an online “airport bus ticket,” check which route and stop it covers before buying.

## Can you catch the first or last airport bus?

Use the **airport column**, not the timetable's headline departure time. In the [800/801 timetable][out800], the early trips marked **x** under **국제공항** do not call at the airport. The airport's first listed services are therefore the ones in the table above.

For [600/601][out600], **06:00 belongs to 601**, while **06:30 belongs to 600**. The route-specific rows show the last airport departures as **22:20 for 600** and **21:02 for 601**. ICC's combined-service summary instead says **22:00**; use the individual Jeju Bus timetable rows for planning and confirm the next departure on the airport display, especially after a late flight.

The [official route guide][busguide] also lists **800-1** as a separate morning/late-night airport–Registry Office service. Check that route separately if your arrival falls outside the table's services; do not assume its schedule is the same as 800 or 801. Allow time for baggage collection and reaching the stop, and have an airport taxi or overnight stay as a fallback if you miss the bus.

## Returning from Seogwipo to Jeju Airport

Open the airport-bound [600/601 timetable][back600] or [800/801 timetable][back800] and read across from your actual boarding stop to **공항 / 국제공항** (airport). **제주터미널** means Jeju Bus Terminal, not the airport. For example, the 801 return sheet lists Registry Office **06:10 → airport 07:22**; that is an arrival at the airport, not a flight departure time.

Check the endpoint before choosing a late return. The final 600/601 rows say **썬호텔 종료** (ends at Sun Hotel), and some late 800/801 rows say **공항미경유** (does not serve the airport). Neither is suitable for reaching the terminal. The 601 return rows begin at ICC, with **X** at earlier coastal stops. For ICC JEJU 1, the venue identifies the airport-bound boarding area as the rotary opposite the convention center. Allow for traffic and your airline's check-in and baggage deadlines.
