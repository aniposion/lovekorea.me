**You can order a seafood meal straight from a restaurant menu near Jagalchi Market.** Start at Busan Myeongmul Hoetjip if you want a set meal. For the experience of choosing your own fish, start at Shindonga Fish Market beside the main Jagalchi building, where you can buy downstairs and arrange to eat upstairs. The addresses look similar in a map app, so save the one that matches how you want to eat.

Official listings checked **November 10, 2026**. Confirm the day's menu and the total with the shop before ordering.

## Which address should you save?

| What you want to do | Destination in Busan's Jung-gu district |
|---|---|
| Order a set or a cooked dish from a menu | Busan Myeongmul Hoetjip (부산명물횟집), **55 Jagalchihaean-ro** |
| Pick fish and arrange for it to be served | Shindonga Fish Market (신동아수산물종합시장), **42 Jagalchi-ro** |
| Browse the main Jagalchi Market building | Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장), **52 Jagalchihaean-ro** |

The [Busan Jung-gu market directory](https://www.bsjunggu.go.kr/eng/index.junggu?menuCd=DOM_000001203005000000) lists Shindonga and Jagalchi as separate stops. The [official Jagalchi visitor guide](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.busan?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301003001000&uc_seq=412) shows walking approaches from Jagalchi and Nampo stations. If your map returns several pins, search by the Korean name and check the street as well as the number.

## Order from a menu if you want a single meal

[Visit Busan lists Busan Myeongmul Hoetjip](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301002002001&uc_seq=1612) as a restaurant at **55 Jagalchihaean-ro**. Its listed *hoe baekban* (회백반) combines sliced raw fish with rice, soup and side dishes. The listing also shows cooked options such as abalone porridge and fish-head stew, which gives a group with different preferences a starting point. Ask what is available that day.

If you're dining alone, show **회백반 1인분 주문할 수 있나요?** to ask whether you can order one portion of the set. Check whether the menu price is per person or per order and what comes with it. If there are two of you and one wants cooked fish, ask whether each person can order a different dish.

## Pick the fish first if you want the market experience

[Visit Busan's Shindonga guide](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000302003001000&uc_seq=2793) describes choosing seafood on the ground floor (1F) and taking it to a restaurant on the second floor (2F) for a seated meal. It also describes eateries on the ground floor, so decide how you want the fish served before choosing a counter.

1. **Get the fish quote for your group.** Point to what you want and ask whether the displayed figure is for that fish or for a unit of weight. Confirm the total for the selection.
2. **Arrange the handoff before paying.** Ask the vendor which upstairs restaurant can serve the fish and whether the quote includes cleaning or slicing. If you want it cooked or want soup, ask what the restaurant can prepare.
3. **Confirm the full meal bill.** Ask which charges belong to the seafood purchase and which belong to the restaurant. Check whether preparation, table service or soup is included, and whether a separate charge is per person or per table.

Show **이 생선을 2층에서 먹으면 손질비와 상차림비까지 총 얼마예요?** to ask what the total will be to eat your fish upstairs, including any preparation and table-setting charges. If the vendor can quote only the fish, check the remaining items with the restaurant before you buy.
