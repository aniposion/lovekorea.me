**Everland’s Plan-it 3 Select starts at ₩27,000, but its three attraction uses do not include Safari World.** They come from a specified list of ride groups. If Safari is your main reason for visiting, buying the cheapest three-use pass will leave that priority visit unresolved. Start with the exact product under **Plan-it** in [Everland’s official catalogue](https://reservation.everland.com/web/el.do?method=productMain).

Checked **October 23, 2026**. Prices below are the catalogue’s **“from” prices**, not confirmed quotes or availability for your travel date. This comparison covers the named official Plan-it products; a seller’s separately named Q-Pass package needs its own inclusion check.

## Three uses can mean two different products

| Product | Listed starting price | What the package allocates |
|---|---|---|
| Plan-it 3 Select | ₩27,000 | 3 uses from selected untimed ride groups |
| Plan-it 3 | ₩32,000 | 1 timed attraction + 2 untimed group uses |
| Plan-it 5 | ₩55,000 | 2 timed attractions + 3 untimed group uses |

The standard [Plan-it 3 product](https://reservation.everland.com/web/el.do?forward_method=getProduct&high_menu_id=0109&menu_id=01090200000000000001&method=getProduct&top_menu_id=01) and [Plan-it 5 product](https://reservation.everland.com/web/el.do?forward_method=getProduct&high_menu_id=0109&menu_id=01090200000000000002&method=getProduct&top_menu_id=01) describe those timed/untimed allocations. **Park admission is a separate purchase.** Each attraction entitlement is for one person’s single use; “3” does not mean a pass shared by three friends.

A timed entitlement also needs a selectable attraction and time on your date. Paying for a package name alone does not confirm your chosen ride.

For autumn visits, the standard 3 page currently lists Royal Jubilee Carousel closed **November 2–December 18**, and Lost Valley **Plan-it sales** paused **September 7–November 30**, subject to change. The latter is a product-sales notice, not a statement that the attraction itself is closed.

## What 3 Select actually covers

On the catalogue, open **Plan-it 3 셀렉트** and its product description. Its listed groups are:

| Group | Attractions in that group |
|---|---|
| A | Hurricane, Bumper Car, Space Tour, Festival Train |
| B | Amazon Express, Royal Jubilee Carousel, Magic Swing, Columbus Adventure |
| C | Thunder Falls, Double Rock Spin, Shooting Ghost, Peter Pan |

Choose three group entitlements; the same group can be selected more than once. Each entitlement lets you use **one attraction within that chosen group**, during its operating hours. It does not unlock every ride in the group. Safari World is absent from all three lists.

**Example itinerary:** you want Safari World, Amazon Express and Thunder Falls. Select can cover the latter two through groups B and C. A third Select use still cannot become Safari. Look for a product that explicitly offers Safari for your date before comparing its final price.

## Count useful rides before calculating value

Use this test for each person: **Does the package cover a wanted ride, can that person ride it, and does the slot fit the day?** Count only entries that pass all three checks.

For a hypothetical ₩27,000 Select purchase:

| Uses you actually want and can take | Effective upgrade cost per useful use |
|---|---|
| 3 | ₩9,000 |
| 2 | ₩13,500 |
| 1 | ₩27,000 |

This is arithmetic, not a prediction of queue time saved. Add admission and transport when comparing the whole outing. If three people all buy at the advertised starting prices, Select totals **₩81,000**, standard 3 totals **₩96,000**, and 5 totals **₩165,000**, before admission. Your date’s actual totals can be higher.

Our recommendation: first match the rides; then compare actual basket totals. A lower price is useful only when the included visits match your plans. A larger package adds value only if you will use its extra entitlements.

## Booking and cancellation details that affect the choice

The standard Plan-it pages checked list booking **7 days before the visit through the previous day**, with limited inventory and no same-day reservation. Membership-benefit versions can have different conditions; use the product you are eligible to buy.

The Plan-it 5 rules require app registration, reject screenshots/printouts for use, and warn that priority access can still involve roughly **30 minutes of waiting**, depending on conditions. A selected 11:00 timed slot means 11:00–11:59. Check ride restrictions before paying. [Read the official product conditions](https://reservation.everland.com/web/el.do?forward_method=getProduct&high_menu_id=0109&menu_id=01090200000000000002&method=getProduct&top_menu_id=01).

For personal changes, its cancellation deadline is the previous day at **23:59 Korean time**, with payment-method exceptions. Same-day cancellation and no-shows are nonrefundable. Operator closures have narrower rules: timed-attraction refunds apply to the affected entitlement, while an untimed group qualifies only if the whole group closes. Do not assume one closed ride refunds the entire package.

## Smart Queue and your account are separate checks

[Everland’s FAQ](https://reservation.everland.com/web/main.do?method=guide&viewPrefix=faq) says registering admission in the app enables Smart Queue attraction/show reservations. Companion management can bring tickets together in one pocket for simultaneous attempts, or transfer them to companions’ apps. That is a different step from buying a Plan-it upgrade.

The FAQ also requires website membership for Smart Reservation. Confirm you can complete the relevant account and payment flow before making a paid slot central to your itinerary. An admission QR code by itself is not a Plan-it reservation.
