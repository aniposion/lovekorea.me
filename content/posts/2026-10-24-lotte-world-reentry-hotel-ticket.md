**A room at LOTTE HOTEL WORLD does not, by itself, give you a leave-and-return park ticket.** For the hotel's **Wonder Chance** benefit, you need an eligible hotel-exclusive ticket from the front desk and must first enter through **Wonder Door** to receive the re-entry wristband. A separately purchased park ticket plus your room key is not enough under the published conditions. Confirm that combination before planning a hotel nap between rides. [Official hotel conditions](https://app.lottehotel.com/world-hotel/en/offers/events/IF230101/wonder-door).

**Official pages checked October 24, 2026.** This guide concerns **Lotte World Adventure in Jamsil, Seoul** and **LOTTE HOTEL WORLD**. It does not extend the hotel's benefit to other Lotte properties, the Busan park or every admission seller.

## Choose the break before buying the ticket

| The break you want | What to establish first |
|---|---|
| Lunch at an ordinary restaurant in the nearby mall | Whether staff authorize your exact outing; the normal park rule does not allow re-entry |
| An afternoon nap in your LOTTE HOTEL WORLD room | Eligible front-desk ticket, first admission through Wonder Door and the issued wristband |
| A visit to Jeojat-geori or the Folk Museum | The park's permitted procedure through the Adventure 3F Folk Museum gate |
| Dinner after you have finished all rides | Treat that exit as the end of your park visit; no return plan needed |

Write the intended break into your itinerary before comparing admission prices. A ticket that works for one continuous visit may be the wrong choice for a family that needs to return to its room.

## Ordinary tickets: leaving normally ends your visit

The park's [official FAQ](https://adventure.lotteworld.com/communication/faq/list), under **“[입장]어드벤처를 나갔다가 다시 들어올 수 있나요?”**, says re-entry is generally unavailable after you exit. It lists these exceptions:

- Using the **ice rink**.
- Visiting **Jeojat-geori or the Folk Museum**, using the **Adventure 3F Folk Museum gate**.
- Visiting an outside medical facility following an assessment by the nurse in the park's infirmary.

Those named exceptions do not establish permission for an unrelated shopping trip or any restaurant near Jamsil. Show your ticket and explain your destination to gate staff **before crossing the exit**. Ask what proof you need, where to return and whether there is a deadline. Do not assume that an old review's stamp or slip procedure still applies to your outing.

For a planned museum visit, also confirm admission eligibility for that facility. A permitted route back into Adventure does not tell you which separately priced activities your ticket includes.

## The hotel benefit requires the right ticket and the right gate

The hotel's [Wonder Door facility page](https://app.lottehotel.com/world-hotel/en/facilities/wonder-door) describes **one same-day re-entry**, during the registered stay, for eligible hotel guests. Its conditions specify hotel-exclusive tickets from the front desk. The wristband is issued when you first enter through Wonder Door; guests entering through the regular main entrance do not receive it there.

Before heading to the park, confirm these details with the front desk:

1. **Ticket:** Does this exact package or ticket qualify for Wonder Chance? A room booking alone does not answer that.
2. **People:** Does every family member who will leave and return have the required admission arrangement?
3. **First entrance:** Where is Wonder Door, and how do we obtain the wristband before our first entry?
4. **Return:** What is today's usable return window, including parade restrictions?

Do this especially on check-in or check-out day, when your room plans and park hours may differ. Keep the ticket and wristband until your park visit is finished.

**Do not count on an early-entry bonus.** The facility page says the scheduled **Wonder Time** early-admission operations for 2026 have concluded. Wonder Door access, Wonder Chance re-entry and Wonder Time early admission are separate benefits.

## Compare the whole family cost

A room near the park and a hotel arrangement with eligible tickets are different purchases. Ask for totals for the **same date, room occupancy, children’s ages and ticket quantities**. Our [family hotel room guide](/posts/korea-family-hotel-room-occupancy/) explains why a room's bed count is not enough to establish occupancy.

**Hypothetical comparison — not a hotel or ticket quote:**

| Option | Assumed family total | Consequence for the plan |
|---|---:|---|
| Nearby hotel ₩180,000 + separately purchased tickets ₩120,000 | ₩300,000 | No hotel re-entry benefit established; plan a continuous park visit |
| Confirmed eligible hotel-and-ticket arrangement | ₩360,000 | A room break can fit if every published condition is met |

In this example, the difference is **₩60,000 for the family**. Decide whether the confirmed room-break option is worth that difference to you. Substitute your actual quotes, add any missing tickets or charges, and check cancellation terms. The example does not claim that either option is currently available or cheapest.

If you can comfortably visit without leaving, compare the ordinary [Lotte World admission windows and Magic Pass options](/posts/lotte-world-tickets-online-vs-onsite/). There is no reason to buy a hotel arrangement solely for a benefit you will not use.

## A useful question at the gate

**“이 티켓으로 나갔다가 다시 들어올 수 있나요? 어디로, 몇 시까지 돌아와야 하나요?”**

“Can I leave and return with this ticket? Which entrance should I use, and by what time?”

Show the actual destination on your phone as well. A clear answer about your ticket and destination is more useful than a general yes-or-no answer about re-entry. If the outing cannot be confirmed, choose food or a rest inside the park, or make the outside meal your final stop after leaving.

**Visiting from November 2026?** The hotel's [September 16 renovation notice](https://app.lottehotel.com/world-hotel/en/about/notice/lotteworld-renewal) covers work on **some park facilities from November 1, 2026 to November 30, 2027**. It is not a notice that the whole park is closing. Check the park's current operating notices for your priority rides before paying for a hotel-and-park package.
