**A salon’s “from” price is not the amount you have agreed to pay.** Before the service starts, confirm the stylist, technique, length charges and add-ons in one total. A cut, down perm and full perm can be separate menu items even when a photo shows them together.

This is a consultation and booking guide, not a personal salon review. We do not publish a citywide price range or treat an unverified branch menu as an official chain tariff.

## Decide what result you want

| Your goal | Service to discuss | What can change the quote |
|---|---|---|
| Shorter length or different layers | Cut | Stylist rank, shampoo and finishing |
| A look for one event | Blow-dry or temporary styling | Hair length, extensions and appointment time |
| Curl or movement after washing | Perm consultation | Technique, hair history, treatment and cut inclusion |
| Less volume at the sides | Down perm or styling consultation | Partial versus full-head service; whether a cut is extra |
| A different shade | Color consultation | Bleaching, number of applications, toner and length |
| Smoother-feeling hair or a relaxation service | Treatment or head spa | Actual steps, duration and optional upgrades |

A trend name such as “C-curl” does not identify one procedure. Explain how long you want the result to last and how you normally style your hair. Let the stylist assess the technique after reviewing your current hair and chemical history.

## Establish the real branch before sending a deposit

For a named starting point, [Seoul Tourism Organization lists A:muu Hair in Myeongdong](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Amuu-Hair/ENPsbsko1), checked October 4, 2026. Use the listed business details to find its current booking channel. A tourism listing identifies an option; it does not establish a live quote, your stylist’s language or our experience of the service.

For any salon, match the branch name, Korean address, floor and contact information. A polished English website using a chain’s name is insufficient by itself to prove the page is operated or approved by that chain. Cross-check it against the brand’s branch directory or the branch’s established channel before paying.

Ask for the consultation in your chosen language for **your appointment**. An English booking form does not guarantee that every stylist can explain a complex color correction in English.

## Get a quote you can actually compare

Send the same brief to each salon you are considering:

1. Date, preferred time and the latest time you must leave.
2. Current hair length and clear front/side/back photographs if you choose to share them through the verified channel.
3. Previous bleach, dye, perm or straightening, with approximate dates.
4. One or two reference photos and what you like about each.
5. Preferred language and your normal home-styling routine.
6. Request for the service list, total, deposit and cancellation deadline.

Ask how much the quote can change after an in-person assessment. Do not treat an estimate from photographs as a promise that every process is possible in one visit. If the plan changes, approve the revised total before the changed service begins.

### Sample bill: why the base price is insufficient

These are invented figures for comparison, **not current salon prices**:

| Component | Salon A | Salon B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Agreed cut and color | ₩150,000 | ₩180,000 |
| Required length charge | ₩30,000 | Included |
| Treatment you decide to buy | ₩40,000 | ₩0 |
| Final agreed total | **₩220,000** | **₩180,000** |

Here A’s lower headline costs ₩40,000 more. But the comparison is only useful if the services and result are genuinely comparable. Remove unwanted optional treatments from both quotes, and confirm whether taxes, shampoo and finishing are included.

A deposit credited against the final bill is a payment already made, not another fee to add twice. Record total, deposit paid and balance due separately.

## Four Korean questions at the chair

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 추가 요금까지 모두 얼마인가요? | What is the total including extra charges? |
| 커트와 샴푸가 포함되어 있나요? | Are the cut and shampoo included? |
| 여기까지만 잘라 주세요. | Please cut only to here; indicate the length. |
| 끝나는 시간이 언제쯤인가요? | About what time will it finish? |

Show the shortest acceptable finished length. Ask the stylist to explain what will happen today, what may need another visit and what maintenance you will need afterward. Confirm before a major cut or an additional chemical process.

## Leave with instructions for your actual service

Ask when and how to wash, what styling is needed and which product precautions apply. Do not use a universal “wait two days” rule for every cut, color and perm. Follow the exact treatment and product instructions. If discomfort occurs during a service, tell the stylist immediately and stop the process for assessment; a head-spa menu is not medical treatment for a scalp condition.

Keep the itemized receipt, agreed service and branch contact. A clear record helps if the result or charge differs from what was discussed.
