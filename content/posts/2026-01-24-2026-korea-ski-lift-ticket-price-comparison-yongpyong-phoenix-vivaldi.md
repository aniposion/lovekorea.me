## How to compare the real price of a ski day

When 26/27 tickets go on sale, price the **same kind of trip** at each resort:

1. **Choose one date and arrival time.** Weekday, weekend and holiday products can differ. Check whether the pass starts at a fixed time or runs for a number of hours after entry.
2. **Match skiing hours.** A 5-hour ticket is not equivalent to an 8-hour daytime ticket. Also check slope maintenance breaks and the latest usable lift time.
3. **Add the right rental.** Check whether you need skis or a snowboard, boots, helmet and clothing. Ask which equipment is included in your selected ticket or lesson, and price missing items separately.
4. **Price the whole journey.** Add your actual train, bus, shuttle or car costs, plus lodging if you are staying overnight. A cheaper lift pass may not mean a cheaper trip from your starting point.
5. **Check the final checkout amount and terms.** Discounts may require a qualifying card, hotel booking or other proof. Confirm the cancellation deadline and what happens if weather changes operations before paying.

For a group, do this once **per traveler**: child and adult prices, rental sizes and lesson needs can differ. Save a screenshot of each final quote with its date, ticket duration and inclusions. That makes a later comparison possible even if promotions change.

## Which resort fits your trip?

- **Yongpyong:** Consider it if slope variety is a priority. The [Korea Tourism Organization's resort guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=251052) describes 28 slopes across skill levels. Confirm which slopes will actually be open on your travel date.
- **Phoenix Snow Park:** Its wide Panorama slope and family facilities make it worth comparing for beginner or family trips, according to the [tourism guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=251052). That is a suitability point, **not evidence that Phoenix has the lowest ticket price**.
- **Vivaldi Park:** This resort is in Hongcheon and has skiing plus family attractions, as the [tourism guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=251052) explains. If traveling from Seoul, calculate the actual door-to-door route and return time for your date before assuming it is the easiest day trip.

The tourism guide lists **2025/26** opening dates and operating hours. Those are historical and should not be used as a 2026/27 schedule. Each resort can adjust opening, slopes and hours with weather and snow maintenance.
