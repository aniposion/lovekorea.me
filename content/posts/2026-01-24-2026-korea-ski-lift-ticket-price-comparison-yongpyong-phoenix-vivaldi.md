**Planning a 2026/27 ski trip to Korea?** As of **September 23, 2026**, we could not verify comparable single-day lift ticket prices for the coming season at Yongpyong, Phoenix Snow Park and Vivaldi Park from their official price pages. That means there is no defensible “cheapest of the three” ranking yet. Use the links below to get a price for your actual date, then compare tickets with the same skiing hours and inclusions.

The figures sometimes circulating online are from earlier seasons. In particular, an older Vivaldi price page still indexed by search engines says **23/24 winter season**. Its prices should not be used as 2026/27 quotes. This guide was checked against resort pages on the date above; resort prices and products can change after publication.

If you need a sense of the previous season's **published list price**, Yongpyong's **25/26** adult daytime lift ticket (09:00–17:00) was **₩96,000** and its adult daytime equipment rental was **₩48,000** on the [resort's labeled rate page](https://yongpyong.co.kr/kor/skiNboard/utilizationFee/rentCharge.do). ## How to compare the real price of a ski day

When 26/27 tickets go on sale, price the **same kind of trip** at each resort:

1. **Choose one date and arrival time.** Weekday, weekend and holiday products can differ. Check whether the pass starts at a fixed time or runs for a number of hours after entry.
2. **Match skiing hours.** A 5-hour ticket is not equivalent to an 8-hour daytime ticket. Also check slope maintenance breaks and the latest usable lift time.
3. **Add the right rental.** Check whether you need skis or a snowboard, boots, helmet and clothing. A lift ticket alone does not automatically include equipment; Phoenix [explicitly separates lift and rental charges for most snow-school programs](https://phoenixhnr.co.kr/en/static/pyeongchang/guide/price/snowpark).
4. **Price the whole journey.** Add your actual train, bus, shuttle or car costs, plus lodging if you are staying overnight. A cheaper lift pass may not mean a cheaper trip from your starting point.
5. **Check the final checkout amount and terms.** Discounts may require a qualifying card, hotel booking or other proof. Confirm the cancellation deadline and what happens if weather changes operations before paying.

For a group, do this once **per traveler**: child and adult prices, rental sizes and lesson needs can differ. Save a screenshot of each final quote with its date, ticket duration and inclusions. That makes a later comparison possible even if promotions change.

## Which resort fits your trip?

- **Yongpyong:** Consider it if slope variety is a priority. The [Korea Tourism Organization's resort guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=251052) describes 28 slopes across skill levels. Confirm which slopes will actually be open on your travel date.
- **Phoenix Snow Park:** Its wide Panorama slope and family facilities make it worth comparing for beginner or family trips, according to the [tourism guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=251052). That is a suitability point, **not evidence that Phoenix has the lowest ticket price**.
- **Vivaldi Park:** This resort is in Hongcheon and has skiing plus family attractions, as the [tourism guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=251052) explains. If traveling from Seoul, calculate the actual door-to-door route and return time for your date before assuming it is the easiest day trip.

The tourism guide lists **2025/26** opening dates and operating hours. Those are historical and should not be used as a 2026/27 schedule. Each resort can adjust opening, slopes and hours with weather and snow maintenance.

## Questions travelers ask

### Which is cheapest: Yongpyong, Phoenix or Vivaldi?

We cannot say yet for the 2026/27 season. First get three **date-specific** prices for equivalent hours, then add the equipment and transport you need. A low headline ticket price may exclude rentals or cover fewer hours.

### Can I use the prices on an older Vivaldi web page?

No. That [official page](https://sonolc.sonohotelsresorts.com/daemyung.vp.skiworld.04_04_01.ds/dmparse.dm) identifies its table as **23/24**. Use Vivaldi's [current rate page](https://www.sonohotelsresorts.com/skiboard/discount) once the coming season's amounts appear.

### Is a Phoenix 26/27 season pass a day-ticket price?

No. Phoenix has announced a [26/27 season-pass product](https://phoenixhnr.co.kr/page/board/event/190000047134). Its price and benefits cannot tell you what a single day at the resort will cost. Compare a season pass only if you expect repeated visits and have read its current conditions.

### When should I recheck?

Check again before booking and again shortly before departure. As of this update, all three resorts still need a verified **26/27 single-day** price for a fair side-by-side comparison. Also check the resort's current slope and transport notices; last winter's timetable is not this winter's timetable.
