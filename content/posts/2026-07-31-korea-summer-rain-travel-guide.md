**Ordinary rain calls for a shorter route; a flood warning calls for a different decision.** An indoor destination is not useful if reaching it means entering a closed underpass or a flooded station. Check conditions before choosing the itinerary, then keep one main stop and a nearby meal.

This is a city-day planning guide, not a forecast for a specific trip. Official sources were checked October 3, 2026. Use live alerts again on the day you travel.

## Make the weather decision first

Start with the [Korea Meteorological Administration's weather and warning service](https://www.weather.go.kr/w/index.do/). Look at your actual destination, not just “Korea” or the airport. Listen to local alerts, transit notices and venue staff.

| What you find | Practical response |
|---|---|
| Showers; transport and chosen venues operating normally | Keep one indoor anchor and shorten exposed walks |
| Persistent rain; visibility poor but route open | Drop viewpoints and waterside detours; use a direct return route |
| Flood warnings, closures or rapidly rising water | Stop the sightseeing plan and follow local evacuation or shelter instructions |
| Unsure whether the route is open | Ask accommodation staff or transport/venue staff before setting out |

Seoul's [flood evacuation guidance](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/living/disaster-evacuation-tips-citizens/flood/) warns against walking into flowing water and driving through flooded areas. Underground does not automatically mean safe in heavy rain. Do not enter flooded passages or bypass barriers to reach a reservation.

## A specific indoor anchor: the National Museum of Korea

For ordinary rain with normal transport, choose the National Museum of Korea as the main destination rather than combining several distant indoor attractions. Its [visitor information](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101010000.do) distinguishes free permanent admission from paid special exhibitions; the Children's Museum requires its own reservation. Check closure dates and final entry before leaving.

The museum also posts [gallery renovation notices](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0606010000.do?arcId=23711&catCustomType=united&catId=128&schM=view). Parts of the painting and sculpture/crafts sections are closed into January 2027. Choose an available gallery from the current map instead of promising yourself a specific room based on an old photograph.

**Suggested half-day structure, not a timed tour:**

1. Travel directly to the museum and use the signed visitor route. Allow for the walk from transit and security; it is not a guarantee of a completely dry journey.
2. Choose two available gallery themes. Spend your attention there instead of trying to finish every floor.
3. Take a meal break at an operating venue you have checked nearby or at the museum. Do not assume every café follows gallery hours.
4. Recheck weather and the route home. An optional second stop can disappear without ruining the day.

For a family, secure any required children's session before making it the reason for the trip. For someone who dislikes museums, replace the anchor with a reserved class or performance and apply the same one-area structure.

## Pack for the failures that matter

| Problem | Small useful item | What it does not solve |
|---|---|---|
| Wet feet after a short walk | Spare socks in a sealed bag | Unsafe water or slippery closed paths |
| A dripping umbrella indoors | Reusable wet bag | Permission to shake water over other people |
| Maps and tickets needed outdoors | Water-resistant pouch and charged battery | A flooded route or a cancelled service |
| Damp clothes in air conditioning | Light dry layer | Exposure during a severe-weather warning |

Wear shoes you already trust for grip. A rain shell may be useful, but quick-drying clothing and a manageable bag usually matter more than carrying a heavy collection of “just in case” gear. Keep charging ports dry and follow the device maker's instructions before charging wet electronics.

## Remove one transfer and one optional stop

Take your dry-weather plan and mark each outdoor segment. Remove the longest optional walk, then remove one cross-city transfer. End near a direct route to your accommodation.

For example, **museum → nearby meal → return** is a complete day. Adding a distant palace, a riverside walk and a night observatory introduces three weather-sensitive decisions. A shorter plan leaves time to enjoy the part that still works.

On transit, close the umbrella before boarding, keep it clear of other passengers and use handrails on wet stairs. Check the intended exit before leaving the platform, but follow staff instructions if that exit is closed.

## If a reservation becomes unusable

Read the cancellation policy and contact the seller through its stated channel. Save the operator's closure notice and your booking details. Weather discomfort, an official closure and a traveller cancelling voluntarily may have different refund terms; do not assume a refund just because rain appears in the forecast.
