**Choose the pier and sailing first, then compare prices for that same cruise.** A lower-priced ticket from the other side of Seoul can lose its saving in transport. A general voucher may also require another step before you have a confirmed departure or boarding ticket.

This guide focuses on E-Land Cruise/이크루즈 planning. Other river operators have their own products and rules. Official pages checked October 3, 2026; use the selected sailing's current details for payment.

## What experience are you buying?

| Your priority | Product detail to look for | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|
| A brief daytime river view | Sightseeing route, duration and return pier | That it includes an evening performance |
| Sunset photography | Date-specific departure and duration | That “sunset” guarantees clear weather or the exact light you want |
| Music after dark | The named performance and route | That music means fireworks, a meal or a fountain show |
| Dinner and sightseeing | Meal location, boarding time and sailing component | That every “dining package” serves the full meal aboard a moving boat |

Start at the [operator's website](https://www.elandcruise.com/). Match the Korean/English product name to the voucher; similar promotional names can conceal different inclusions.

## Yeouido and Jamsil are not interchangeable

For the current autumn period, the [operator's Jamsil notice](https://www.elandcruise.com/CsCenter/NoticeDetail.aspx?BrdIdx=174&BrdNo=701101) specifies weekend sailings from **August 1 through October 31, 2026**. That is a dated operating notice, not a permanent rule that Jamsil always runs every weekend throughout the year.

Use your actual ticket's pier address and terminal instructions. A map pin for “Han River Park” is too broad. Plan the final walk to the ticket office and the correct boarding point, not merely the journey to a subway station.

If Jamsil fits your hotel but no usable sailing exists, compare a Yeouido trip against a different local evening. You do not need to cross the city solely to preserve a cruise idea.

## Purchase, reservation and boarding are three checks

The operator's [boarding-process FAQ](https://www.elandcruise.com/CsCenter/FaqDetail.aspx?BrdIdx=150&BrdNo=701102) describes visiting the ticket office and completing boarding formalities. Its [identification FAQ](https://www.elandcruise.com/CsCenter/FaqDetail.aspx?BrdIdx=77&BrdNo=701102) lists accepted identification, including passports, and specific conditions for minors. Read the current instructions for your party rather than assuming a QR code alone completes everything.

Save the following before leaving your accommodation:

1. **Purchase:** seller, order number and the exact product bought.
2. **Reservation:** date, departure time, pier and whether the seller has confirmed them.
3. **Boarding:** required identification, ticket-exchange process and check-in deadline.

The operator's [September–December 2026 visitor instructions](https://www.elandcruise.com/Event/Eventing.aspx?EvtIdx=133&Type=D) advise reaching the ticket office at least 15 minutes before departure and state that ticket issuance closes five minutes before departure. Allow additional time for an unfamiliar route; 15 minutes is not a promise that all queues will clear. Follow a stricter deadline if your specific booking has one.

## Compare the whole evening

There is no single verified “Han River cruise price” that covers sightseeing, music, dining and special-event products. Do not carry January prices or a past flash sale into an October booking.

Use this **hypothetical comparison**, replacing the numbers with live quotes:

| Two-adult evening | Nearby departure A | Distant departure B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tickets | ₩60,000 | ₩50,000 |
| Additional transport for this plan | ₩6,000 | ₩24,000 |
| Comparable total | **₩66,000** | **₩74,000** |

Here the ticket that looks ₩10,000 cheaper costs ₩8,000 more overall. Compare equivalent routes and cancellation terms before using this calculation. Meals and optional purchases belong in additional rows if they differ between plans.

## Rain does not automatically mean cancellation

The operator's current visitor instructions say sailings can operate in rain, while river-authority restrictions may cause cancellations. Check the actual operating notice. Choosing not to sail and the operator cancelling can produce different refund outcomes.

A Banpo fountain view also depends on the route and the fountain's operation. The [city's Banpo information](https://hangang.seoul.go.kr/www/contents/663.do?mid=463) describes seasonal service; a cruise name cannot guarantee the show. Avoid buying a ticket whose value depends entirely on an unconfirmed extra.

## Finish with the journey home

Add sailing duration, disembarkation and the walk out of the park before checking your last convenient transit connection. For accessibility needs, ask the operator about the specific vessel and boarding method before purchase; “family-friendly” is not an accessibility specification.
