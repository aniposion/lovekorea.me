**Recognizing 최애 and 컴백 helps you follow a fan conversation. Recognizing 신청, 당첨 and 본인 확인 helps you understand what an event notice is asking you to do.** A vocabulary guide cannot establish the rules for your concert, but it can help you find the section that matters before spending money.

All sample sentences below are invented for learning. They are not a current event announcement, song lyrics or instructions from an artist.

## Start with fan conversation

| Term | Useful meaning in this context | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 최애 | Favorite person or thing; often “bias” in a group discussion | 최애 곡이 뭐예요? — What's your favorite song? |
| 차애 | A next or second favorite in fan usage | 차애도 있어요. — I have a second favorite too. |
| 입덕 | Becoming a fan | 그 무대를 보고 입덕했어요. — I became a fan after that performance. |
| 덕질 | Activities done as a fan | 요즘 덕질이 재미있어요. — I'm enjoying being a fan these days. |
| 컴백 | In K-pop discussion, a new release or promotion period | 컴백 소식을 봤어요. — I saw the comeback news. |
| 응원법 | A cheering or fanchant guide | 응원법을 연습하고 있어요. — I'm practicing the fanchant. |

The [National Institute of Korean Language's entry for 최애](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=601213) covers the most-loved thing or person, not only an idol member. Do not turn it into a compulsory ranking of every member, or assume it always means one worldwide “ultimate bias.”

## Then find the action in the notice

| Notice word | What to look for next | A question to resolve |
|---|---|---|
| 신청 — application | Opening and closing times | Is this an application or a confirmed booking? |
| 당첨 — selection/winning | Result and confirmation steps | Have I actually been selected? |
| 예매 — ticket reservation | Official seller and ticket category | What exactly am I buying? |
| 선예매 — advance/presale booking | Eligibility and verification instructions | Does this specific presale require membership? |
| 본인 확인 — identity verification | Accepted documents and name requirements | Which original document must I bring? |
| 입장 — entry | Gate, time and ticket instructions | When and where can I enter? |
| 취소 / 환불 — cancellation/refund | Deadline and conditions | What happens if my plans change? |
| 응원봉 — lightstick | Allowed version and pairing instructions | Is my model supported at this event? |

These translations locate questions; they do not answer them for an unnamed event. Read the organizer's complete dated notice and any changes. A screenshot from a past tour may have different rules.

## A fanchant guide is a separate resource

For a concrete example, SEVENTEEN's [official Weverse notice for the “LOVE, MONEY, FAME” fanchant](https://weverse.io/seventeen/notice/22969) is identified as an 응원법 guide. It shows that this resource is song-specific. Follow the artist's own guide for timing instead of assuming every song starts with the same name order.

Use the recording or official guide to practice short sections. You can enjoy the performance without mastering every chant. Venue rules on phones, signs and other equipment still apply independently. This article does not reproduce lyrics or promise a setlist.

## Questions you can show staff

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 입장 줄이 어디예요? | Where is the entry line? |
| 이 줄이 맞나요? | Is this the correct line? |
| 여권을 보여 드리면 되나요? | Should I show you my passport? |
| 예매 내역은 어디서 확인하나요? | Where can I check my booking details? |
| 다시 말씀해 주시겠어요? | Could you say that again? |
| 영어 안내가 있나요? | Is there information in English? |

Ask these of identifiable event staff at the official location. Do not send identity-document images to an unknown reseller because a message includes the words 본인 확인.

## Make one event note before booking travel

Write the official event link, performance date and time zone, venue address, ticket status, required documents, cancellation deadline and transport home in one note. Record “not yet confirmed” for a missing detail rather than filling it from another fan's event.

Build the budget from the actual ticket and selected extras. A lightstick, membership or merchandise purchase is not automatically necessary for admission; the particular notice controls. This guide gives no “typical 2026 concert price” because unnamed events and packages are not comparable.
