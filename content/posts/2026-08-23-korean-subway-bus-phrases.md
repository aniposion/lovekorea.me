**Need to check a platform, recharge a card, or recover from a missed stop?** Save the Korean text below and point to your destination on a map. A clear place name helps more than a long sentence spoken from memory.

These are 25 polite phrases for buses and subways. Romanization is an approximate reading aid. The examples are language practice, not directions for a particular journey.

![Illustration of a traveler asking for help at a subway station](/images/korean-subway-bus-phrases-h2-0.webp)

## 25 phrases to save before your journey

| # | Korean | Reading aid | Meaning / when to use it |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 저기요. | Jeogiyo. | Excuse me; get someone's attention. |
| 2 | 도와주세요. | Dowajuseyo. | Please help me. |
| 3 | 천천히 말해 주세요. | Cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo. | Please speak slowly. |
| 4 | 여기 가고 싶어요. | Yeogi gago sipeoyo. | I want to go here; show the map pin. |
| 5 | 이 역이 맞아요? | I yeogi majayo? | Is this the right station? |
| 6 | 이 버스가 맞아요? | I beoseuga majayo? | Is this the right bus? |
| 7 | 서울역에 가요? | Seoullyeoge gayo? | Does it go to Seoul Station? |
| 8 | 어디에서 타요? | Eodieseo tayo? | Where do I board? |
| 9 | 몇 호선을 타요? | Myeot hoseoneul tayo? | Which subway line do I take? |
| 10 | 어느 방향이에요? | Eoneu banghyangieyo? | Which direction is it? |
| 11 | 어디에서 갈아타요? | Eodieseo garatayo? | Where do I transfer? |
| 12 | 몇 번 출구예요? | Myeot beon chulgu-yeyo? | Which exit number? |
| 13 | 엘리베이터가 어디예요? | Ellibeiteoga eodiyeyo? | Where is the elevator? |
| 14 | 이 정류장이 맞아요? | I jeongnyujangi majayo? | Is this the right bus stop? |
| 15 | 몇 정거장 남았어요? | Myeot jeonggeojang namasseoyo? | How many stops are left? |
| 16 | 다음 정류장에서 내려요. | Daeum jeongnyujangeseo naeryeoyo. | I'm getting off at the next stop. |
| 17 | 여기에서 내려도 돼요? | Yeogieseo naeryeodo dwaeyo? | May I get off here? Ask when stopped. |
| 18 | 교통카드 충전하고 싶어요. | Gyotongkadeu chungjeonhago sipeoyo. | I'd like to top up my transit card. |
| 19 | 이 카드가 안 돼요. | I kadeuga an dwaeyo. | This card isn't working. |
| 20 | 잔액이 얼마예요? | Janaegi eolmayeyo? | How much balance is left? |
| 21 | 추가 요금이 있어요? | Chuga yogeumi isseoyo? | Is there an additional fare? |
| 22 | 반대 방향으로 탔어요. | Bandae banghyangeuro tasseoyo. | I boarded in the opposite direction. |
| 23 | 정류장을 지나쳤어요. | Jeongnyujangeul jinachyeosseoyo. | I missed my stop. |
| 24 | 막차가 몇 시예요? | Makchaga myeot siyeyo? | What time is the last service? |
| 25 | 얼마나 걸려요? | Eolmana geollyeoyo? | How long does it take? |

For number 7, replace **서울역** with your destination. Keep the Korean place name visible: **서울역** (Seoul Station) and **서울** (the city) are different requests. For number 24, show your destination too; the final departure from a station may terminate before your stop.

## What to check before boarding

| If you are using… | Check these together | A useful phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Subway | Line number, direction, destination and transfer station | 어느 방향이에요? |
| City bus | Bus number, side of the road and stop list | 이 버스가 맞아요? |
| Airport transport | Terminal, service type and ticket conditions | 여기 가고 싶어요. |
| A route with luggage | Elevator location and final walk to the address | 엘리베이터가 어디예요? |

An exit number is not necessarily an accessible route. Follow elevator signs when needed. On a bus, use the stop button in time and wait for a designated stop; a phrase asking to get off does not authorize the driver to stop anywhere.

The [Seoul public-transport guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/public-transportation/) and [KTO transportation-card guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140663) explain fare media and transfers. Check the current rules for your route instead of assuming a transfer discount applies to every airport, intercity or regional service. Keep your card ready and follow entry/exit tapping instructions.

## Three practice situations

**Wrong platform:** Show your destination and say **반대 방향으로 탔어요. 여기 가고 싶어요.** You have explained the mistake and the place you want to reach. Ask station staff before leaving a paid area; do not climb barriers or follow someone through a gate.

**Card failure:** Say **이 카드가 안 돼요.** Let staff or the machine identify the problem before adding money repeatedly. A balance problem, wrong ticket type and card-reading problem need different solutions.

**Unclear directions:** Say **천천히 말해 주세요**, then point to the station name or exit on the map. Confirm one step at a time. A line number without a direction and destination is incomplete advice.

## Signs worth recognizing

- **입구 / 출구** — entrance / exit
- **타는 곳 / 내리는 곳** — boarding / alighting area
- **환승** — transfer
- **충전** — top-up
- **막차** — last service
- **운행 종료** — service ended

Listen to word pronunciations in the [National Institute of Korean Language's Korean Basic Dictionary](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/mainAction?nation=eng). Our sentences are practical examples; the dictionary is a language reference, not the source of a specific transit route.
