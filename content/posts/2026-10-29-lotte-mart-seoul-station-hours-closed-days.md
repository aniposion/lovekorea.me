**Plan your Lotte Mart Seoul Station visit around a 23:00 (11 PM) closing time.** [Lotte Mart's own store finder](https://global.lottemart.com/stores) currently lists **10:00–23:00**, although official tourism pages still show midnight. The branch's regular closures are the **second and fourth Wednesdays**, according to [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/Lotte-Mart-Seoul-Station/ENP000541). Check both the date and the closing time before leaving snack shopping until your last evening.

**Official pages checked October 29, 2026. All times below are Korea Standard Time (KST).** This guide covers **ZETTAPLEX Seoul Station**, not every Lotte supermarket, outlet or department store.

## Why use 11 PM when another guide says midnight?

The disagreement is visible in the official sources:

| Source checked | Listed hours |
|---|---|
| [Lotte Mart's store finder](https://global.lottemart.com/stores), Seoul Station card | 10:00–23:00 |
| [Visit Seoul's branch page](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/Lotte-Mart-Seoul-Station/ENP000541), edited September 10, 2026 | 10:00–24:00 |
| [Korea Tourism Organization's branch page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=145561) | 10:00–00:00 |

Use the **operator's earlier time for planning** and reconfirm with the branch if a late visit is essential. These pages do not establish when the hours changed. A screenshot from an older itinerary is not enough to resolve the difference.

The branch contact published by the tourism offices is **+82-2-390-2500**. Ask about your exact date, especially if you need more than ordinary shopping.

## Check the branch's closure calendar

On the date checked, the operator's Seoul Station card listed October 3, 14 and 28 as closed. Its Jamsil card listed October 3, 11 and 25. That difference is why a general Sunday-closure reminder for Korean supermarkets is a poor substitute for the actual branch calendar.

For a future visit, open [Find Stores](https://global.lottemart.com/stores), choose **Seoul**, and locate **ZETTAPLEX Seoul Station**. Read the closure dates on that card. Make sure the displayed month matches your trip; a list of October dates does not confirm November opening.

**Planning example for November 2026:** the second and fourth Wednesdays fall on **November 11 and 25**. Those dates are calculated from the regular rule published by Visit Seoul. They are **not a separately confirmed November holiday notice**; check the operator's new monthly list for exceptions before traveling.

## How much time does your arrival leave?

These examples are simple clock calculations using the listed 23:00 closing time **on an open day**. They are not estimates of checkout speed or guarantees of admission.

| Arrival at the supermarket | Time until 23:00 | Practical decision |
|---|---:|---|
| 21:30 / 9:30 PM | 90 minutes | A planned list gives you room to compare items and pack afterward. |
| 22:30 / 10:30 PM | 30 minutes | Keep the list short; do not build the visit around unconfirmed counter services. |
| 23:15 / 11:15 PM | Already 15 minutes past | Move the shopping trip earlier instead of relying on a midnight listing. |

Your train's arrival at Seoul Station is **not** your arrival at the supermarket. Allow for leaving the platform, finding the store and handling your bags. After shopping, you still need time to pay, repack and reach the correct departure platform.

If you have a fixed onward train, work backward:

**Latest time to leave the shop = train departure − your platform-access allowance − your bag-retrieval or repacking allowance.**

Choose those allowances for your luggage, mobility and familiarity with the station. The store's closing time does not extend your train ticket. Use the [AREX guide](/posts/incheon-airport-arex-guide/) for the airport leg; shopping time should fit around the journey you can actually take.

For an early flight or tight departure day, buy gifts on a previous day. For a late arrival, arrange the few essentials you need tonight first and leave a large souvenir basket for a less rushed visit.

## Find the supermarket and check the service you need

Search for **롯데마트 제타플렉스 서울역점**. [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/Lotte-Mart-Seoul-Station/ENP000541) lists **405 Hangang-daero, Jung-gu, Seoul** and **Seoul Station Exit 1 on subway Lines 1 and 4**. Follow the route from your actual platform; that exit reference is not a walking-time promise from AREX or KTX.

The same tourism listing mentions **lockers and EMS international delivery**. It does not give current locker sizes, charges or shipping-counter cutoffs. If either service is essential, ask the branch before building the day around it. Keep a separate [Seoul luggage-storage option](/posts/seoul-luggage-storage-guide/) in your plan, including its retrieval deadline.

For shopping tax refunds, Lotte's [own checkout instructions](https://global.lottemart.com/tax-free) include passport scanning and separate immediate-refund and later-refund paths. Bring your passport and ask which method applies before paying. Keep the refund documents if the refund will be claimed later; our [Korea shopping tax-refund guide](/posts/korea-tax-refund-shopping-guide/) explains the wider process.

Before filling your bag with food gifts, check how you will carry them home. The [kimchi packing guide](/posts/kimchi-plane-sealed-packs-liquids/) helps with the cabin-versus-checked-bag decision, while the [U.S. food-entry guide](/posts/korean-snacks-us-customs/) is for readers flying to the United States.

## Save this before you go

- **Exact branch:** ZETTAPLEX Seoul Station / 롯데마트 제타플렉스 서울역점.
- **Your visit date:** compare it with the current monthly closure card.
- **Your departure deadline:** allow for checkout, bags and reaching the platform.
- **Any essential service:** confirm its own availability rather than assuming it runs until the store closes.
- **Your shopping list:** decide what you need before arriving late, and leave space in your luggage.
