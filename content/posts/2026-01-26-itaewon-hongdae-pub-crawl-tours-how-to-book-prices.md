**Choose the crawl whose route and return journey suit you—not the one promising the most drinks.** Hongdae and Itaewon are separate nightlife areas. A cheap ticket is less useful if the final stop leaves you far from your accommodation or the venue rules do not fit your group.

This is a booking comparison for adult travellers, not a tested ranking of seven tours. Operator pages checked October 3, 2026. Availability and the date-specific ticket conditions need another check when you book.

## One operator, two areas: what is actually published

The Seoul Pub Crawl publishes separate [Hongdae](https://www.seoulpubcrawl.kr/hongdae/) and [Itaewon](https://www.seoulpubcrawl.kr/itaewon/) pages. They describe a hosted route with three bars and one club, entry benefits, a shot at each venue and drink discounts. These are the operator's advertised inclusions, not our independent assessment of the experience.

The operator says venues change and directs guests to the date-specific ticket page for the first meeting place. Its FAQ lists photo identification with a birth date, the confirmation QR code, a dress code and a 19+ participation context. Check any further venue restrictions with the organiser; the crawl label is not permission to ignore a door policy.

Neither neighbourhood guarantees a particular crowd, music style or age mix. Ask about the actual night's route if those details determine whether you would enjoy it.

## Use this comparison before choosing

| Question | Why it changes the decision |
|---|---|
| Where is the first bar for my date? | An old meeting address can send you to the wrong place |
| Where does the hosted part end? | The final location matters more than the first for getting home |
| Are entry fees included at every named stop? | “Discounted access” and included admission are different |
| Can I decline drinks or choose a non-alcoholic substitute? | An included shot is optional consumption; substitution may not be included |
| What if a venue refuses entry or changes? | You need the organiser's actual substitution/refund policy |
| Can I leave early, and how do I contact the host? | You should not have to finish the route to get home independently |

For someone staying in Hongdae, a Hongdae route can simplify the end of the evening. For someone already dining in Itaewon, an Itaewon route may fit better. Check the final stop and transport instead of relying on broad claims that one district is always cheaper or more welcoming.

## Compare the total, without invented market prices

Record the current ticket quote, optional purchases and the journey home. There is no verified universal ₩30,000–50,000 market rate here.

**Illustrative example:** a ₩30,000 ticket plus ₩25,000 of return transport costs ₩55,000 before extra purchases. A ₩40,000 ticket with a ₩5,000 return costs ₩45,000. The second ticket costs more, but this traveller's evening costs ₩10,000 less. Replace those sample amounts with your own route estimates.

Do not assign a saving to drinks you would not otherwise buy. If you want one quiet conversation over dinner, a single venue may fit better than paying to move with a group.

## Save a short joining note

Before leaving, save the operator's name, date, first venue address, permitted joining window, host contact and confirmation. Read the actual clothing and ID rules. If two friends bought through different sellers, confirm they are joining the same organiser and session.

Agree on an independent return plan and keep enough phone battery to use it. A host can organise a route, but does not guarantee personal safety or transport home. Keep control of your drink and belongings, respect others' boundaries, and leave if the crowd or situation feels unsafe. Drinking is optional; do not treat finishing included drinks as getting your money's worth, and do not drive after drinking.
