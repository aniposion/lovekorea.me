**Buy a separate ITX-Cheongchun ticket before boarding for Gapyeong.** Tapping your T-money card into the subway does not purchase that ticket. If you want a journey paid through your transit card, choose the **Gyeongchun Line subway service** instead. Both can get you to Gapyeong for Nami Island, but they have different ticket and boarding procedures.

This guide checks the official sources on **October 23, 2026**. Start with the train name in your route planner, then check the departure station. Seeing “Gapyeong” on a map is not enough to identify which service you are taking.

## Which train should you choose?

| Your priority | Start by comparing | What to prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel without booking a particular seat | Gyeongchun Line subway, commonly reached at Sangbong | T-money balance and the correct train direction |
| Reserve a seat for a particular departure | ITX-Cheongchun from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni | A separate ticket for the date, stations and train you will board |
| Leave Nami Island whenever you finish | Subway return, checked against that day's timetable | Time to get back to Gapyeong Station; no reserved seat |
| Keep a fixed return appointment in Seoul | A suitable ITX return departure | A booked return ticket and enough time for the ferry and station transfer |

Nami Island's [official return-travel FAQ](https://namisum-en.imweb.me/35/?bmode=view&idx=7167500) distinguishes the subway's unreserved seating from ITX's reserved-seat option. A seat is only yours when your issued ticket actually assigns one. If the departure you want is unavailable, compare another train or the subway before committing to an attraction time.

T-money is a reloadable local transport card; the [Korea Tourism Organization's card guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140663) explains its use. If your card is a WOWPASS, check the [separate shopping and T-money balances](/posts/wowpass-tmoney-separate-balances/) before topping up the wrong one.

## Buy the ITX ticket for the right station

Use [KORAIL's official English booking site](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main). Search **Yongsan → Gapyeong** or **Cheongnyangni → Gapyeong**, according to the station you can reach comfortably. Check the service says **ITX-Cheongchun**, then review the date, departure time, passenger details, seat and fare before payment.

[KORAIL's current ticketing guide](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/passengerGuide/ticketTypes/tickets) lists its website, KORAIL+ app, ticket machines and railway station counters as purchase channels. Its [ITX-specific FAQ](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttList.do?bbsNo=205&key=817&pageIndex=5) clarifies that ordinary subway stations which do not sell passenger-train tickets cannot issue ITX tickets. Look for the ITX machine or railway ticket counter rather than assuming any subway machine will sell one.

Check your return separately: **Gapyeong → your intended Seoul arrival station**. A return ticket ending at Cheongnyangni leaves you with a different onward journey from one ending at Yongsan.

## Transferring from the subway to ITX

There are two things to handle: **finish the subway fare transaction** and **have a valid ITX ticket**.

1. **Already inside the subway system?** Follow signs for the ITX transfer and use the designated transit-card exit/transfer reader to close your subway journey before boarding ITX. If you have already tapped out through a normal subway exit, that subway journey is finished; do not tap in again just because you see a card reader.
2. **Starting from outside the paid area?** Have your ITX ticket ready and follow the ITX entrance signs. Where there is a QR gate, scan the ticket's QR code at the QR reader. A T-money reader and an ITX QR reader serve different purposes.
3. **Check the train before boarding.** Match the service, train number and departure time to your ticket. Go to your assigned car and seat if you have a reserved-seat ticket.
4. **At Gapyeong, follow the ITX exit signs.** If you are uncertain which reader applies, show both the ticket and your transit card to station staff before tapping again.

The [government's May 2017 transfer explanation](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=148833342) describes the subway-exit reader used before an ITX transfer. [KORAIL's February 2023 QR-gate notice](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=199&key=911&nttNo=18074) explains the separate ticket-QR reader. These sources establish the procedure, not a guaranteed reader position at every station today. Follow current signs and staff directions rather than searching for a platform number from an old photo.

Useful phrase to show staff: **“ITX로 갈아타는데, 교통카드 하차 처리는 어디서 하나요?”** — “I'm transferring to ITX. Where do I tap out my transit card?”

On the return, entering the subway after ITX begins a subway journey that needs its own transit-card entry. If you take the subway all the way back instead, follow the normal subway tap-in and tap-out procedure.

## Compare the whole journey, not just the train minutes

Nami Island's [official access page](https://namisum-en.imweb.me/164) publishes these approximate station-to-station times:

| Rail leg to Gapyeong | Published approximate ride |
| --- | ---: |
| Gyeongchun Line from Sangbong | 50 minutes |
| ITX-Cheongchun from Cheongnyangni | 42 minutes |
| ITX-Cheongchun from Yongsan | 60 minutes |

These are planning estimates, not a departure timetable. **“42 versus 50 minutes” does not mean ITX saves eight minutes from your hotel:** the two figures begin at different stations. Likewise, the Yongsan figure includes a longer starting route. Compare journeys from the same hotel departure time.

For each option, add:

- Hotel → departure station, including walking and transfers.
- Waiting time and enough time to find the correct entrance.
- The actual train journey shown for your travel date.
- Gapyeong Station → Nami Island's wharf, then the island crossing and any queue.

**Example of the method, not a live itinerary:** a 42-minute train with 25 minutes to reach it and 20 minutes waiting totals **87 minutes to Gapyeong**. A 50-minute subway leg with 15 minutes to reach it and 5 minutes waiting totals **70 minutes**. The shorter rail ride would arrive 17 minutes later in this hypothetical comparison. Replace every input with your own route and departure time.

For cost, compare **the subway journey's total fare** with **the subway-to-station fare plus the ITX ticket**. Add the wharf transfer and island entry to both budgets. The ITX ticket gets you to Gapyeong Station; your day-trip budget still needs those later parts. Our [Nami Island ferry, zip-wire and day-trip comparison](/posts/korean-day-trips-guide-2024/) covers the island-entry choice.

If you are combining Gapyeong with longer rail journeys, use the [KORAIL Pass cost worksheet](/posts/korea-ktx-korail-pass-prices-2026/) and check each service's eligibility separately. A local transit-card balance and a rail-pass purchase are different budget items.
