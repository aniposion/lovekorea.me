**A hanbok jacket worn with jeans does not qualify for free palace admission.** The [Korea Tourism Organization's visitor guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1589263) also flags a regular T-shirt paired with a hanbok bottom. Check the complete outfit before paying for a rental or packing a single piece from home.

Official guidance checked **October 25, 2026**. This guide addresses clothing eligibility for ordinary palace admission, using the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center's rules. Special events and separately ticketed areas need their own checks.

## Check the complete outfit

The [official hanbok guideline and illustrated FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R705000000.do) accepts both traditional and modern everyday hanbok when the clothing requirements are met. Foreign visitors are eligible too.

| Outfit you plan to wear | Published clothing rule |
|---|---|
| Jeogori jacket with a hanbok skirt or pants | The required basic combination; check the garment shapes |
| Jeogori with jeans | Does not qualify |
| Hanbok skirt or pants with a regular T-shirt | Does not qualify |
| A durumagi overcoat alone | Does not replace the upper-and-lower outfit |
| A one-piece hanbok dress without a jeogori | Does not qualify |

The jeogori must retain its overlapping collar; a particular ribbon-knot method is not required. Pants should follow the traditional *sapok* form. Skirt styles are flexible, but a waist skirt needs a jeogori reaching the waist. Use the official illustrations for those details, especially when evaluating a modern design.

**Our practical check:** lay out or try on everything you intend to wear together. A product name such as “modern,” “premium” or “palace style” cannot show you whether a required piece is missing.

## Choose among packing, renting and ordinary admission

| Your starting point | Useful next step | What to avoid buying on assumption |
|---|---|---|
| You already own a complete outfit | Compare the actual garments with the official illustrations before packing | A replacement rental merely because your outfit is modern |
| You own one piece or want to mix it with everyday clothes | Decide whether you want a complete outfit for the experience, or prefer your own clothes and the applicable admission ticket | An accessory upgrade that leaves the missing garment unresolved |
| You plan to rent | Try the complete selection and confirm the total quote before committing | A package whose attractive photograph shows pieces excluded from your price |

For an unusual garment, ask the palace about the exact outfit before deciding what to pack or rent. Compare the [palace ticket options](/posts/korea-historic-palaces-guide/) before deciding how much the admission benefit matters to you.

## Before the rental clock starts

Use this short fitting-room checklist:

1. **Confirm the included pieces.** Ask the shop to identify the jacket and skirt or pants in your chosen price. Separate optional styling from the clothes you will actually wear.
2. **Try your planned combination.** Check fit and movement with your intended layers and footwear. Ask how your own clothes will be stored while you are out.
3. **Write down the return deadline.** Establish whether changing time is included and what happens if you return late. Build the trip back to the same shop into your route.
4. **Keep the outfit decision separate from the outing.** Check palace opening arrangements, your planned visit area and any reserved session before choosing the rental duration.

For a hypothetical four-hour rental, a one-hour allowance for changing, walking and returning leaves three hours for the visit. Adapt this example to your shop's actual terms and your own walking pace.

## A useful question at the shop

> **이 가격에 저고리와 치마 또는 바지가 모두 포함되어 있나요?**
>
> Does this price include both the jeogori and a skirt or pants?

If you also want Changdeokgung's Secret Garden, plan its [separate ticket and session](/posts/changdeokgung-secret-garden-tickets-sold-out/). The [official fee page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R703000000.do) separates garden admission from palace-building admission. Do not use an ordinary free-entry benefit as a substitute for checking a garden booking or a seasonal event notice.
