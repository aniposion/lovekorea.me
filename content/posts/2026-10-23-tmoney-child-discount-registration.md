**Buying and loading a T-money card does not, by itself, register your child's fare discount.** Have the child's birth date registered before relying on the lower fare. For Seoul's ordinary subway card base fare, the published difference is **₩550 for a child versus ₩1,550 for an adult**. A card handed down from someone else needs an additional check: changing its registered birth date can take **three working days** to affect the fare.

These figures and registration steps were checked **October 23, 2026**, against [Seoul's subway fare table](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/subway/) and [Tmoney's current registration guide](https://pay.tmoney.co.kr/ncs/pct/ugd/ReadDcRgtMthdGd.dev). This guide covers **physical T-money cards** for family trips.

## Match the child's age to the fare category

[Tmoney's English visitor guide](https://pay.tmoney.co.kr/ncs/pct/ugd/ReadFrgnGd.dev) defines children as **ages 6–12** and youth as **ages 13–18**. It says to present the card and identification when registering the birth date at a convenience store or subway information center.

| Passenger category | Ordinary Seoul subway card base fare |
|---|---:|
| Adult | ₩1,550 |
| Youth, ages 13–18 | ₩900 |
| Child, ages 6–12 | ₩550 |

**Scope:** these are the current amounts in Seoul's table effective June 28, 2025, not a nationwide price for every bus or train. Distance, route and other fare conditions can change the total. For a child under six, check the operator's free-accompaniment and seat rules rather than selecting a category from this table. Intercity trains have separate rules; see our [KTX infant and child-seat fare guide](/posts/ktx-toddler-infant-child-seat-fare/).

## Ask for registration when buying the card

The [Korea Tourism Organization's card guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140663) tells young visitors to register their date of birth when purchasing a card. Make that a separate request from adding money to the balance.

Have the **physical card and age identification** ready. For an overseas child, carry their passport. You can show staff this request:

> 아이 생년월일을 등록해서 어린이 할인을 적용해 주세요.
>
> Please register my child's date of birth for the child discount.

For a teenager, replace **어린이 할인** with **청소년 할인**. Ask staff to confirm that the birth date was recorded, then label the card so the right family member keeps using it.

Tmoney's [registration instructions](https://pay.tmoney.co.kr/ncs/pct/ugd/ReadDcRgtMthdGd.dev) distinguish three routes:

| Card or purchase route | Registration step |
|---|---|
| All-age card at GS25, CU, 7-Eleven, Emart24 or a listed subway customer center | Birth-date registration at purchase completes the process. |
| All-age card from another seller | Set the child/youth fare type, then register the birth date within 10 days of first use. |
| Designated child/youth card | Register the birth date online within 10 days of first use. |

Missing required registration means ordinary fares. **Do not interpret the deadline as ten automatic days of discounted travel.**

Online registration has a **non-member child/youth option** requiring the card number and birth date. Follow through to the confirmation.

## A borrowed card has a different timing problem

For a handed-down card, update the birth date online, at a convenience store or a listed subway center. Tmoney specifies **three working days** before the corrected discount applies.

For a short holiday, compare a new card's purchase cost with the extra fares during that wait. Confirm timing with staff before choosing either option.

## What the difference means for a family budget

Here is a **calculated example**, not a ticket quote: two adults and two eligible children each make four separate journeys charged at the ordinary Seoul subway base fare above.

| Calculation | Total |
|---|---:|
| Two adults × four journeys × ₩1,550 | ₩12,400 |
| Two children × four journeys × ₩550 | ₩4,400 |
| Family total with the child fares | **₩16,800** |
| Same 16 passenger journeys all charged at ₩1,550 | **₩24,800** |
| Difference in this example | **₩8,000** |

This excludes card purchases, distance supplements, early-morning discounts and transfers treated as part of one journey. Use your planned routes for the real budget. An adult's pass comparison also needs recalculating for a child: our [Climate Card versus T-money guide](/posts/seoul-climate-card-vs-tmoney/) explains the cost categories, but its adult-fare examples are not a child's break-even calculation.

## Check the setup before your first sightseeing day

- **Keep each card identifiable.** Use a name label or different sleeve so an adult does not accidentally pick up the child's registered card.
- **Keep phone and physical-card instructions separate.** If an adult uses mobile T-money, a separate physical card for the child keeps that child's registration easy to identify. Do not assume these plastic-card steps describe Apple Wallet or another app's registration process.
- **Question an unexpected fare with the route details.** A charge above the base fare can reflect the journey, not necessarily failed registration. Ask staff to check both the card category and the trip before changing the setup again.
- **Treat unused balance separately from fare spending.** Our [T-money refund guide](/posts/tmoney-refund-balance-store-limits/) helps you choose a refund counter by the remaining balance before leaving Korea.

The useful order is **buy the right card, register the child's birth date, confirm when the discount applies, then budget the journeys**. Loading more money cannot fix the card's age category.
