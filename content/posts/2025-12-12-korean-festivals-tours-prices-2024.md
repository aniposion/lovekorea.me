**A festival name and a season are not enough to book a trip.** Confirm the year, your particular performance, its venue and the journey home. A festival can run for eleven days while the activity you want happens on only two of them.

This guide uses official 2026 examples checked October 3, 2026, then shows how to evaluate another event. It is not a complete festival calendar; past dates below are labelled so they cannot be mistaken for upcoming availability.

## Example 1: Andong has several venues and separate programs

The organiser's [2026 Andong International Maskdance Festival overview](https://en.maskdance.com/2024/sub1/sub1.asp) lists **September 24–October 4, 2026**, with activity around Central Line 1942 Andong Station, downtown and Talchum Park. The URL contains “2024,” but the page's event heading and dates identify the 2026 edition. Check the displayed content rather than judging by the URL alone.

The [Hahoe Village program page](https://en.maskdance.com/2024/sub2/sub1_7.asp) names another venue. Do not confuse a downtown festival stage, Hahoe Village and the railway station where your train arrives. Save the address of your actual performance and build the local transfer around it.

The organiser also lists [separate tours for international visitors](https://en.maskdance.com/2024/sub3/sub3.asp). For example, the Hahoe Village Seonyu Julbulnori Premium Tour is advertised for September 26, October 3 and October 4 at **US$27 per person**, with a minimum group requirement. This is one named tour's published offer, not the price of general festival admission or proof that seats remain available. Read its detailed inclusions and operating confirmation before booking.

If reading this after October 4, those 2026 dates have passed. Wait for the next edition's confirmed program rather than carrying them forward by a year.

## Example 2: Seoul Light DDP is an edition with dates

[Seoul's official announcement](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2019251) scheduled the **2026 autumn edition for September 3–13**, with free viewing from 19:30 to 22:30. That edition has already ended as of this guide's check date.

It does not support a claim that the festival runs continuously from December through February. A later light display, recurring installation or winter edition needs its own notice. “DDP is open” and “the festival performance is running tonight” are different checks.

For a future city evening, look for the exact show date, viewing zone, cancellation notice and last public transport. A ticket seller offering a city tour may be selling transport or guiding around a free event; compare what the paid product actually adds.

## Build an event card before buying anything

Save these seven fields in one note:

1. **Edition and dates:** the year and full event period.
2. **Your activity:** the named performance, workshop or session.
3. **Venue:** the actual entrance or meeting point, not just the city.
4. **Start and finish:** including the arrival window and time to leave the crowd.
5. **Admission:** free access, ticket, reservation, paid seat or separate activity charge.
6. **Operating conditions:** weather changes, minimum group and cancellation terms.
7. **Return plan:** the last practical connection and a fallback.

A useful question for the organiser is: “Is this activity operating at this venue on this date, and what ticket does each person need?” It is more precise than asking whether “the festival” is free.

## Budget for the whole event day

Use **transport + local transfers + admission/activities + food + any necessary overnight stay**. There is no meaningful nationwide festival budget without those choices.

**Illustrative example:** return travel of ₩50,000, local transfers of ₩10,000 and food of ₩20,000 make a free event an ₩80,000 outing. A paid ₩15,000 event near your accommodation might cost less overall. These figures demonstrate the method and are not live fares or event prices.

For a tour, compare the same performance and return time. A bundle that leaves before the finale may be unsuitable even if it includes more daytime stops. Confirm whether meals, workshop materials and reserved seating are included rather than assuming they come with a festival ticket.

## When to keep the plan flexible

For outdoor shows, leave room for weather and crowd management. For workshops, protect the booked session and avoid a tight onward train. For winter activities, do not infer operating conditions from last year's photographs. Use a confirmed current notice before paying for equipment or a distant hotel.
