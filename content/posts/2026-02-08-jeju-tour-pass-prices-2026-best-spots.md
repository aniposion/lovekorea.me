**A Jeju Tour Pass is not automatically a rental-car package.** Buy it only after the attractions and benefits you genuinely want exceed the pass price and any extra travel costs. A long list of cafés does not make a pass valuable if reaching them consumes your sightseeing day.

This guide explains the decision, rather than promising a universal pass price or a fixed list of included attractions. Official sources checked October 3, 2026.

## First, separate the products

[LS Company's Tourpass information](https://www.lscompany.co.kr/company/tourpass) describes a mobile ticket for participating attractions and experiences, with remaining time and venue information. Its [mobility service](https://www.lscompany.co.kr/company/mobility) separately describes rental-car comparison and booking. A company offering both services does not mean your attraction ticket includes a car.

The [official purchase and use guide](https://tourpass.ai/tourpass-guide/) links to [Tourpass Mall](https://www.tourpassmall.com/national-tour-pass). Product duration, venue benefits, reservations and refunds vary. Open the particular Jeju product you intend to buy and save its terms; a company-wide partner logo is not proof that a venue is included in that product.

| Wording on the product | How to value it |
|---|---|
| Admission included | Count the admission you would otherwise purchase, subject to its restrictions |
| Discount | Count only the discount, not the entire attraction price |
| Selected drink included | Count that drink only if you would order it |
| Reservation required | Count it after confirming a usable session |
| Rental-car offer | Read a separate vehicle booking and insurance total; do not assume inclusion |

## The break-even worksheet

Use **avoided spending − pass price − additional travel/required extras**. Avoided spending means something you would have bought without the pass. It is not the sum of every retail price in a brochure.

Here is an **illustrative example, not current Jeju pricing**:

| Planned item | Standalone amount | Value counted for this traveller |
|---|---:|---:|
| Museum they already wanted | ₩16,000 | ₩16,000 |
| Reserved performance they wanted | ₩18,000 | ₩18,000 |
| Coffee on their existing route | ₩5,000 | ₩5,000 |
| Extra photo stop added only for the pass | ₩10,000 | ₩0 |
| Free public museum | ₩0 | ₩0 |

Their relevant value is ₩39,000. A hypothetical ₩30,000 pass with ₩4,000 of additional travel costs saves **₩5,000**. If the performance is unavailable, the remaining ₩21,000 of value produces a **₩13,000 loss** against that same total. That is why confirming one important reservation can matter more than collecting many minor perks.

The [Jeju National Museum](https://jeju.museum.go.kr/html/en/) lists free general admission, with charges possible for special exhibitions. Do not count a free general visit as money saved by a pass.

## The activation trap

The official guide distinguishes products timed from first use from products tied to particular dates. Check yours. For a pass that runs for 48 continuous hours from activation, starting at 3 p.m. Monday means expiry at 3 p.m. Wednesday—not Wednesday closing time. This is a clock example, not a promise about every product.

Before the first scan, write down:

- The expiry method and any gaps required between redemptions.
- Each priority venue's opening hours and last admission.
- Whether repeated visits, upgrades or accompanying children cost extra.
- Which reservations are confirmed, and who handles changes.

Do not activate merely to test the barcode. Follow the ticket's redemption instructions when you are ready to use the first benefit.

## Keep the route on one side of Jeju

Mark your actual included stops on a map. Start with the attraction you most want, add a nearby meal, then one additional stop that fits the available time. A Seogwipo stay does not become cheaper because a small included benefit pulls you across the island.

For a day dominated by Hallasan, the Udo ferry or free coastal walking, run the arithmetic with those priorities first. Do not assume the pass includes mountain access, ferry transport or famous landmarks simply because they appear in a generic Jeju itinerary.

If the pass makes the route worse, buy individual admission. If it covers a compact route of wanted, available stops, the convenience may be worthwhile even when the cash saving is modest.
