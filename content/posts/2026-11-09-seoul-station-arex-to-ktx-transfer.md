**At Seoul Station, follow the KTX or railway transfer signs from the AREX arrival level to the Korail concourse.** Keep your airport train ticket or transit card handy through the exit gates, then match your KTX train number to the departure board before going to the platform. When you book the two journeys separately, measure the connection from the **scheduled AREX arrival at Seoul Station** to the **KTX departure**. Add time for the walk and elevators, plus a buffer for a late airport train.

Official sources checked **November 9, 2026**. All example times are in **Korea Standard Time (KST)**. The time budgets below are illustrative.

## From the airport train to the departure board

1. **Get off at Seoul Station and look for the railway transfer signs.** AREX's [station map](https://www.arex.or.kr/file/emergency_seoul.pdf) places its platforms on B7. The map shows separate ticket areas for the Express and All-Stop services on higher basement floors. You'll go up from the train, then cross toward the main railway concourse. Follow the current signs in the station, especially if a passage or lift is out of service.
2. **Use the ticket or card you used for AREX at its exit.** Keep the separate KTX ticket ready on your phone or on paper. AREX has an [official KTX transfer walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iYeR1bAasuc) you can review before travel. Its video dates to 2021, so use the current wayfinding at the station for the last turn.
3. **Match the train number and departure time in the Korail hall.** A destination on the display may be farther down the route than your own stop. The train number is the useful identifier. [Korail's station FAQ](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttList.do?bbsNo=205&key=817&pageIndex=12&searchCnd=all) says platform numbers can change and directs passengers to station displays and announcements. If you have a KorailTalk ticket, the FAQ says the ticket view can show the platform starting 15 minutes before departure.
4. **Follow the current platform assignment and find your car.** Check the platform again if the board changes. With luggage, leave time to reach the right car after you arrive on the platform.

If you need an elevator, ask at the point where you need to go up. Show staff **공항철도에서 KTX 타는 곳까지 엘리베이터로 어떻게 가나요?** to ask how to reach the KTX boarding area from AREX by elevator.

## Budget time for three parts of the connection

| Part of the journey | What to allow time for |
|---|---|
| Airport to the AREX platform | Immigration, bags, the walk to rail and waiting for a train |
| Inside Seoul Station | Leaving AREX, using lifts or escalators, reaching the Korail board and getting to the KTX car |
| Extra time | A late arrival, a lift queue, a platform change or time with station staff |

When you're comparing departures, keep the first line separate from your time inside Seoul Station. A bigger station buffer does little for a flight delay if you still can't reach the AREX train on which your plan depends.

**Worked example.** Your planned AREX arrival at Seoul Station is **14:05**. A **14:20** KTX leaves **15 minutes** at the station. Suppose your own plan allows **20 minutes** for the transfer and **15 minutes** of contingency. That gives a total of **35 minutes**, so your example cutoff is **14:40**. A **14:45** departure, if available, fits the example. A 15-minute airport rail delay would leave **25 minutes** at the station on that later option.

Use a larger allowance if you are walking slowly, need a lift, have children or want to stop before boarding. The example is a way to choose a train using your own assumptions.

## Check the departure station on your KTX ticket

Confirm the ticket says **Seoul Station (서울)** if you plan to make this connection. If it says **Yongsan (용산)** or **Gwangmyeong (광명)**, plan a separate onward trip to that station. Check the station and train number when you buy through [Korail's official booking site](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main). Our [Seoul Station versus Yongsan guide](/posts/ktx-seoul-station-or-yongsan/) explains why the departure station matters.

If the airport train is running late, check the change options for your KTX ticket while you still have time. The issuer controls changes, fees and what seats you can move to. If you bought two independent tickets, check both bookings separately. See the [missed KTX guide](/posts/missed-ktx-train-refund/) for the questions to ask your seller.

### Service note for Friday, November 13, 2026

AREX [published a contingency plan on November 6](https://www.arex.or.kr/contentView.do?contentNo=CT202611060000000002&etcNo=&menuNo=MN202602270000000001) after a warning strike was announced for November 13. The plan calls for normal Express service and near-normal All-Stop service in the morning and evening commuting windows, with fewer All-Stop trains planned outside those windows. Recheck the operator's current notice and your exact train before relying on that timetable for a KTX connection.
