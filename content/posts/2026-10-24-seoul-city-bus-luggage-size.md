**A carry-on accepted by your airline can still be too big for a Seoul city bus.** Article 17 of the [Seoul city-bus terms](https://www.sbus.or.kr/2018/law/04.htm?20260220=) sets a **per-person weight below 20kg** and dimensions **below 50 × 40 × 20cm**. Check the actual packed bag before choosing a route with a city-bus connection.

**Checked October 24, 2026**, against the terms effective February 20, 2026. This guide concerns ordinary luggage on **Seoul-licensed city buses**. The [bus association's scope notice](https://www.sbus.or.kr/2018/faq/faq.htm) distinguishes village buses and buses licensed in Gyeonggi or Incheon; their conditions can differ. Airport coaches have their own baggage rules.

## Why a light suitcase can still fail

Weight and dimensions are separate checks. The Seoul terms use **미만**, meaning *less than*. Do not treat a bag exactly on a stated boundary as automatically accepted. Items that could block aisles or doors are also restricted under Article 10; Article 12 permits refusal of passengers carrying those items.

These are **hypothetical packed-bag examples**, not approved products. Dimensions include the outside of the bag as you will carry it; retract the pull handle and account for protruding wheels, handles and expanded pockets when measuring.

| Example bag, all weighing 8kg | What the size check shows |
|---|---|
| 55 × 35 × 19cm | The 55cm side exceeds 50cm |
| 49 × 39 × 21cm | The 21cm depth exceeds 20cm |
| 49 × 39 × 19cm | Below the listed dimensions; this is not a boarding guarantee |

A shop's “20-inch” or “cabin size” label is not a substitute for measuring. Write down the three outside measurements and packed weight, including the other bags you intend to carry.

## Identify the bus before applying the rule

A map showing a bus to the airport does not tell you that it is an airport coach. Likewise, a short bus connection after an airport train still needs its own luggage check.

The [Seoul bus association's route guide](https://www.sbus.or.kr/2018/bus_info/bus_01.htm) identifies blue trunk buses and green feeder buses, among other city-bus categories. Use the **route number and operating company**, not color alone, to identify the service.

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport's [May 7, 2026 baggage explanation](https://www.korea.kr/multi/visualNewsView.do?newsId=148963991) separates city buses, airport buses and express/intercity buses, and warns that regional conditions differ. A luggage hold on one service does not establish permission to bring the same suitcase into another bus's passenger area.

For a route-specific question, the [association's FAQ](https://www.sbus.or.kr/2018/faq/faq.htm) points passengers to [Seoul TOPIS](https://topis.seoul.go.kr/map/openBusMap.do) to identify the route and bus-company contact details. Give the company the route, travel direction, bag count, outside measurements and weight.

## Check the last connection to your hotel

Before selecting the fastest-looking itinerary, write each leg on a separate line. Here is an **illustrative planning comparison**, not a live timetable or price quote:

| Proposed route | The decision to resolve |
|---|---|
| Airport train → city bus → hotel | Check the suitcase against the bus conditions before relying on the last leg |
| Airport train → walk → hotel | Check the actual station exit, lift access, walking surface and distance with luggage |
| Airport coach → walk → hotel | Confirm that operator's baggage allowance, stop and remaining walk |
| Taxi or transfer → hotel | Confirm space for the whole group and every bag before booking |

Suppose your first plan is a train followed by a six-minute city-bus ride. If the suitcase cannot go on that bus, the six-minute connection is not a usable saving. Compare a suitable walking route, an alternative rail connection or a correctly sized vehicle **from the station**, as well as a direct transfer from the airport.

## If the driver cannot take your suitcase

Keep the doorway clear and move to a safe place to choose your alternative. Do not make catching the next identical service your only plan: another arrival time does not change the dimensions of your bag.

A short question before boarding is:

> 이 캐리어를 가지고 탈 수 있나요?\
> Can I board with this suitcase?

Show the bag rather than relying on the phrase “small suitcase.” If the explanation is unclear, note the route, stop, direction and time so the operating company can understand the question later. A previous successful ride is not confirmation for every future journey.

If you only need a day bag for sightseeing, compare [Seoul luggage storage options](/posts/seoul-luggage-storage-guide/) before choosing a locker or counter. Verify that the bag fits and that you can collect it before closing; storage adds a return trip to your itinerary.

**Before leaving your room:** measure and weigh the packed luggage, identify every bus in the route, save the destination's Korean address, and keep a workable alternative for the last connection.
