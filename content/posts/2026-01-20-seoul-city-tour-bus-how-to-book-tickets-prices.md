# Seoul City Tour Bus: Tickets, Prices, Routes & Booking

**Checked September 25, 2026.** This guide covers two different products sold by the [official Seoul City Tour Bus operator](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/tours): the **Downtown, Palaces & Namsan** daytime hop-on, hop-off loop and the **Han River & Namsan** night ride. A ticket for one is not a ticket for the other. Other sightseeing bus operators may have different routes and fares.

The quick decision: buy the **day course** if you want to get off at several central-Seoul landmarks. Choose the **night course** for a single 90-minute ride with one N Seoul Tower photo stop. If you only need transport between specific neighborhoods, compare the subway instead.

| Official course | What the ticket does | Starting price shown on the operator's English site* |
|---|---|---|
| [Downtown, Palaces & Namsan](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/tours/1) | Hop on and off at 15 stops on the ticket day | Adult **US$22**, child **US$16** |
| [Han River & Namsan Night Tour](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/tours/2) | One 90-minute circuit; one approximately 25-minute photo stop | Adult **US$20**, child **US$14** |

\*These were the **“from” prices displayed on September 25, 2026**, not a guarantee for every date, currency, or option. Check the operator's booking page and the **final checkout total** before paying.

## Daytime route: what the pass covers

The [official daytime course page](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/tours/1) lists **15 stops**, including Myeong-dong, Namsangol Hanok Village, N Seoul Tower, Dongdaemun Design Plaza, Changdeokgung, Insa-dong/Bukchon, Cheong Wa Dae, and Gyeongbokgung. The full loop takes about **90 minutes without getting off**. From Gwanghwamun, the published first and last departures are **09:20 and 16:50**, normally about **25 minutes apart**.

The pass lets you reboard this course throughout its valid day, but **seats are not reserved**. If the next bus is full, you may have to wait for another. An open-top double-decker is **not guaranteed**; the operator may use a glass-top double-decker or semi-open single-decker. Traffic and events can change arrival times.

The course normally closes **Mondays**. If Monday is a public holiday, the operator says it runs Monday and closes **Tuesday instead**. Read the [latest operating notices](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/notices) for your travel date: temporary road controls have closed stops or shortened service on particular days.

## Night route: a different ticket

The [official night course](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/tours/2) is **not hop-on, hop-off**. It is a roughly **90-minute round trip** with one approximately **25-minute stop at N Seoul Tower**. Its published departure is **19:00 from September through April** and **19:30 from May through August**, from the Gwanghwamun ticket office. It also normally closes Mondays, with the same holiday-Monday exception.

There is one departure per operating evening. The operator advises arriving **at least 40 minutes early** to exchange your booking for a boarding pass and queue; seats are first come, first served. Its [FAQ](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/support/faq) says the night course needs at least **10 customers** to operate. Confirm availability before planning an evening around it.

## How to book and board

1. Pick the **day** or **night** course on the [operator's tours page](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/tours). Check the route, date, currency, and total before checkout.
2. **Book online by the previous day.** The operator says same-day online booking is unavailable. For a same-day **daytime** ticket, buy at the Gwanghwamun booth if seats remain. For the **night** course, call the operator first at **+82-2-777-6090** to check remaining seats.
3. Go to the **Gwanghwamun ticket office**, 68-2 Taepyeong-ro 1-ga, Jung-gu, near Koreana Hotel. The operator places it about **100 m from Gwanghwamun Station Exit 6** on Line 5, or **300 m from City Hall Station Exit 3**.
4. If you booked online, give staff the **name on the reservation** and collect your boarding pass. Show that pass to the driver. Arrive early if you hope to sit upstairs; the pass does not reserve a particular seat or bus type.

For these courses, the operator applies the **child fare from 48 months through age 18**. A child under 48 months may ride free **without a seat**, limited to **one per accompanying adult**. Check the exact conditions on your chosen course before purchasing. Attraction admission, meals, and other paid stops are separate unless a particular ticket explicitly includes them.

## Which option is worth the money?

The **day pass** is most useful when you plan to stop at two or more places on its central loop and value the elevated views and multilingual audio guide. It is a poor substitute for a direct trip to Hongdae, Gangnam, Jamsil, or another destination outside that loop. The **night ride** is for the view and commentary, not for transport between stops.

For a cost comparison with ordinary transit and the short-term Climate Card, see our [city tour bus versus subway guide](/posts/seoul-city-tour-bus-vs-subway/). If you have a timed palace or show reservation, leave a buffer or take the subway: the tour bus can be delayed by traffic and road closures.

Before paying, read the [operator's cancellation FAQ](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/support/faq). Its refund depends on how close the departure is, and the night course has a same-day cutoff. Check the policy shown at your own checkout and the [current notices](https://www.seoulcitybus.com/en/notices) again shortly before riding.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I book online on the day I ride?** No. The operator directs same-day daytime buyers to the Gwanghwamun booth, subject to availability, and asks prospective night riders to call first.

**Does a daytime ticket guarantee an open-top seat?** No. Seats are first come, first served, and bus type can vary.

**Can I hop off the night bus and board later?** No. The night tour is one circuit with an N Seoul Tower photo stop. Hop-on, hop-off applies to the daytime course.

**Does the day pass include palace or tower admission?** The standard course describes the bus ride and audio guide. Budget separately for attractions unless the specific ticket you buy explicitly includes them.
