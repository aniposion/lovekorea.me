**Official booking information checked October 22, 2026; guided-tour example checked September 25, 2026.** You have two practical ways to plan the controlled Paju DMZ sightseeing course: use [Paju City's official reservation information](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) and arrange your own journey to Imjingak, or book a Seoul-departure tour that handles transport and guiding. Neither route guarantees access: security and weather can close the course with little notice.

## Choose your booking route

| | Paju official reservation | Seoul-departure guided tour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best if | You can arrange transport to Imjingak and prefer to manage the booking yourself. | You want a guide and organized travel from Seoul. |
| Where to book | Use Paju's [English reservation site](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Eng/index), then **Real-time Reservation**. | Check the selected operator's live itinerary and checkout terms. |
| Guide | Paju says its official course does **not** supply a separate guide. | A guide is included only when the particular tour listing says so. |
| Price | Check the official reservation and ticket information for your date; allow separately for travel to Imjingak. | Check the final price for your date and option; extras and refund terms vary. |
| Main trade-off | More planning and no included guide. | Less transport planning, but a fixed schedule and the operator's change policy. |

The public **Imjingak area** and the **controlled DMZ Peace Tourism course** are different plans. A visit to Imjingak alone does not mean you have a ticket for the Third Tunnel or Dora Observatory. Likewise, a product called “DMZ tour” does not automatically include the Joint Security Area (JSA). Check the actual stop list before paying.

## How to use Paju's official reservation

1. Open Paju's [English reservation site](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Eng/index) and choose **Real-time Reservation** to reach the [date and session calendar](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Eng/E40000/E40100/bookingIndividual). Select the date, course and session before entering party details. Check the route, fees and notices for that date. This booking starts at Imjingak; arrange your Seoul-to-Imjingak transport separately.
2. Plan your own travel to **Imjingak** and allow time for every person in your party to arrive. Paju says it can issue tickets only after **all members are physically present** and their identities are checked.
3. Bring an accepted **original, valid ID**. For foreign visitors, Paju lists a passport, alien registration card or US military ID. A photo or screenshot of a document, or an expired passport, is not accepted.
4. Check the day's operation before leaving Seoul. Paju lists closure on **Mondays**, weekday public holidays and the day of Seollal or Chuseok. It says on-site and reservation ticket issuance normally runs **09:00–14:30**, but can end earlier when sold out. Military conditions, weather and safety concerns can cancel even an advance reservation.

Paju's course does not provide a guide, and the city lists restrictions on entering the steep, narrow Third Tunnel for some visitors with mobility or health limitations. If that applies to your group, contact the [DMZ Peace Tourism ticket office](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) before booking rather than assuming a different tunnel entry method is available.

## If the online session is full

**An online sellout does not automatically mean that all walk-up tickets are gone.** Paju's [English booking calendar](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Eng/E40000/E40100/bookingIndividual) says on-site purchase may still be possible, depending on that day's ticket stock. It does not promise a place for your party.

| What you see | Your next step |
| --- | --- |
| Your preferred online session is full | Compare another session or date. For a walk-up attempt, check the official site's ticket status or call the ticket office before committing to the journey. |
| You have an online reservation | Check your confirmation, bring everyone's original ID and arrive together for ticket issuance. A reservation does not replace the identity check. |
| The course is closed for security, weather or safety | Choose a different day or a Seoul backup plan. A reservation or walk-up attempt cannot override a closure. |

For a walk-up visit, Paju's [ticket-office instructions](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Home/H30000/H30100/boardView?board_key=514), published August 29, 2026, give this order: **arrive at the Imjingak ticket office → check remaining tickets → scan the office's QR code and enter visitor details → purchase and collect tickets**. QR registration alone is not the ticket. The [visitor guide](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Eng/E20000/E20100/html) lists the ticket office numbers as **031-954-0303** and **031-954-0640** and warns that tickets often sell out early.

For online bookings, double-check the date, session and each visitor's details before payment. The [official reservation guide](https://dmz.paju.go.kr:8443/Eng/E20000/E20400/html) says detailed information cannot be edited after payment. Use the site's **Reservation check/Cancel** route for an existing booking; check its terms before canceling or buying another ticket.

**Photography:** Paju's published rule prohibits photo and video recording, including drones, on its DMZ Peace Tourism course. Follow the current signs and staff instructions; do not rely on photos in a tour advertisement as permission to shoot at a particular stop.

## Compare the actual cost and risk

Avoid a fixed “typical DMZ tour price” in old guides: a headline price may refer to a different departure, pickup point or bridge option. To compare fairly, check the **final checkout total** for your date, number of travelers, included admission, transport, guide language, meals, optional stops and cancellation terms. For the official route, add the cost and time of getting yourself to Imjingak.

Before paying for any guided tour, ask these five questions:

1. Does my **selected option** include the Third Tunnel and Dora Observatory, or only Imjingak and other stops?
2. Where exactly do we meet, and where are we dropped off in Seoul?
3. Is the bridge or gondola an included stop, an optional upgrade, or absent?
4. What happens to my payment if I cancel, and what happens if the military closes the intended route?
5. Can everyone in my group bring accepted ID and manage the walking, slopes and tunnel?

Leave your return schedule flexible. Paju says security or weather can interrupt access without advance notice, even when a reservation was accepted.
