# Seoul DMZ Tour Booking Guide: Official Tickets or a Guided Trip?

**Updated September 25, 2026.** You have two practical ways to plan the controlled Paju DMZ sightseeing course: use [Paju City's official reservation information](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) and arrange your own journey to Imjingak, or book a Seoul-departure tour that handles transport and guiding. Neither route guarantees access: security and weather can close the course with little notice.

The public **Imjingak area** and the **controlled DMZ Peace Tourism course** are different plans. A visit to Imjingak alone does not mean you have a ticket for the Third Tunnel or Dora Observatory. Likewise, a product called “DMZ tour” does not automatically include the Joint Security Area (JSA). Check the actual stop list before paying.

## How to use Paju's official reservation

1. Open [Paju City's DMZ Peace Tourism page](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) and follow its current reservation link. Check availability, the route, fees and notices for your date. This is the city's booking route, not a Seoul hotel pickup service.
2. Plan your own travel to **Imjingak** and allow time for every person in your party to arrive. Paju says it can issue tickets only after **all members are physically present** and their identities are checked.
3. Bring an accepted **original, valid ID**. For foreign visitors, Paju lists a passport, alien registration card or US military ID. A photo or screenshot of a document, or an expired passport, is not accepted.
4. Check the day's operation before leaving Seoul. Paju lists closure on **Mondays**, weekday public holidays and the day of Seollal or Chuseok. It says on-site and reservation ticket issuance normally runs **09:00–14:30**, but can end earlier when sold out. Military conditions, weather and safety concerns can cancel even an advance reservation.

Paju's course does not provide a guide, and the city lists restrictions on entering the steep, narrow Third Tunnel for some visitors with mobility or health limitations. If that applies to your group, contact the [DMZ Peace Tourism ticket office](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) before booking rather than assuming a different tunnel entry method is available.

**Photography:** Paju's published rule prohibits photo and video recording, including drones, on its DMZ Peace Tourism course. Follow the current signs and staff instructions; do not rely on photos in a tour advertisement as permission to shoot at a particular stop.

## Compare the actual cost and risk

Avoid a fixed “typical DMZ tour price” in old guides: a headline price may refer to a different departure, pickup point or bridge option. To compare fairly, check the **final checkout total** for your date, number of travelers, included admission, transport, guide language, meals, optional stops and cancellation terms. For the official route, add the cost and time of getting yourself to Imjingak.

Before paying for any guided tour, ask these five questions:

1. Does my **selected option** include the Third Tunnel and Dora Observatory, or only Imjingak and other stops?
2. Where exactly do we meet, and where are we dropped off in Seoul?
3. Is the bridge or gondola an included stop, an optional upgrade, or absent?
4. What happens to my payment if I cancel, and what happens if the military closes the intended route?
5. Can everyone in my group bring accepted ID and manage the walking, slopes and tunnel?

Leave your return schedule flexible. Paju says security or weather can interrupt access without advance notice, even when a reservation was accepted.

## FAQ

**Can I go without a tour company?** Yes. Start with [Paju's official reservation page](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004), then arrange your own travel to Imjingak and follow the ticket and ID requirements. The official course does not include a guide.

**Is a phone photo of my passport enough?** No. Paju explicitly rejects document photos and screenshots. Bring an accepted original, valid ID.

**Is a DMZ tour the same as a JSA/Panmunjom tour?** No. The example product above lists the Third Tunnel and Dora Observatory, not JSA. Treat JSA as a separate itinerary and verify any access claim on the exact booking page.

**Can a confirmed booking change?** Yes. Paju can cancel its course because of security, weather or safety conditions. The guided product above can substitute a route under its stated conditions, with no refund for that change. Read the operator's current terms before paying.
