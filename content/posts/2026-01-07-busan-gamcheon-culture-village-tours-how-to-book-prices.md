# Gamcheon Culture Village: Free Visit or Guided Tour?

**Updated September 25, 2026.** You do **not** need a ticket or a tour to walk around Gamcheon Culture Village. [Visit Busan lists admission as free](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=365), apart from parking. Pay for a guide only if you want someone to explain the neighborhood's history and take you between several Busan stops.

## Know the 2026 visiting rules first

Gamcheon is a residential hillside neighborhood, not an open-air theme park. Busan's [official attraction listing](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=365) gives visitor hours of **09:00–18:00 from March through October** and **09:00–17:00 from November through February**. The village's [special management area guide](https://gamcheon.or.kr/gamcheon/api/user/intro1-content/pdf/en/download) also restricts tourists in designated residential areas outside **09:00–18:00**. In winter, plan around the earlier 17:00 closing time. Check the village's current notice before your visit.

Keep voices low, leave doorways and narrow lanes clear, and do not photograph into homes. Visit Busan also says **no drones**. The [village residents' association](https://gamcheon.or.kr/gamcheon/page/user/intro1) explains that this is still a place where people live; its Korean War refugee history is part of the visit, not just a backdrop for pictures.

## How to visit without a tour

**Address:** 203 Gamnae 2-ro, Saha-gu, Busan.

The [Visit Busan directions](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=365) give two Metro Line 1 approaches:

1. From **Toseong Station Exit 6**, transfer to village bus Saha 1-1, Seo-gu 2, or Seo-gu 2-2.
2. From **Goejeong Station Exit 6**, transfer to village bus Saha 1 or Saha 1-1.
3. Get off at **Gamjeong Elementary School** and walk about five minutes to the village.

Check the stop and bus route on your travel day. The hillside has steps and slopes, so allow time to walk slowly. Public transport also avoids the parking problem at this busy residential site.

For a simple self-guided visit, start at the entrance information center and small museum to learn the village's history. Follow the marked public lanes to the viewpoints and art, then return by the same transit route. Visit Busan says a stamp map can be purchased at the information center; it is optional, not an admission ticket. Bring shoes with grip and leave room for residents to pass at photo spots.

**Cost:** Village entry is free. Budget separately for your bus fare, food, souvenirs, optional map or activities, and parking if you drive. There is no reason to buy a “Gamcheon entry ticket” for a normal walk.

## FAQ

**Is Gamcheon Culture Village free?** Yes. Visit Busan lists entry as free except for parking; optional activities and purchases cost extra.

**Do I need to reserve a time slot?** Not for a normal independent walk. Paid tours have their own departure times and booking rules.

**Can I visit after dark?** Do not plan a tourist walk through the residential lanes after the permitted visiting window. Check the [special management area guide](https://gamcheon.or.kr/gamcheon/api/user/intro1-content/pdf/en/download) and the current village notice before traveling.

**Is Songdo Cable Car included in the tour above?** No. Its listing includes Songdo Skywalk on the itinerary and excludes cable car fare. See our [Songdo Cable Car guide](/posts/busan-songdo-cable-car-tickets-prices/) if you plan to ride it separately.
