Dragging a suitcase through Seoul after hotel checkout is rarely a good use of your last day. The better choice depends on four things: bag size, storage time, where you will finish sightseeing, and whether you are willing to return to the same place.

**Prices, locations, dimensions, and operating details in this guide were checked on September 5, 2026.** The main source is the Seoul Metropolitan Government's current English [subway storage facilities guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/public-transportation/subway-storage-facilities/). Availability, acceptance rules, and same-day delivery cutoffs can change, so confirm the live service before building a flight connection around it.

## Seoul Luggage Storage Options at a Glance

![Traveler comparing carry-on and full-size suitcases beside station lockers and service counters](/images/seoul-luggage-storage-guide-h2-0.webp)

There is no single “Seoul luggage storage” system that fits every trip. Subway lockers are self-service compartments. T-Luggage is a staffed storage and delivery service at selected stations. A hotel may hold bags for guests, while airport operators provide their own counter services. These options have different hours and require different return plans.

| Option | Best use | Main advantage | Main limitation | Published price example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-Locker subway locker | A few hours with a bag that fits | Many station locations and self-service access | App and locker instructions may be Korean; compartments can sell out | Weekday four-hour base: KRW 2,200 small, KRW 3,300 medium, KRW 4,400 large |
| T-Luggage staffed counter | Full-size luggage or help from staff | Size categories extend to XL and a person can check the bag | Only selected stations; counter hours are narrower than the subway day | Weekday four-hour base: KRW 3,000 small to KRW 9,000 XL |
| Hotel or guesthouse storage | Before check-in or after checkout | Often the least complicated route when returning to the property | Not guaranteed; policies may exclude non-guests, long holds, or late pickup | Ask the property directly; this guide does not assume a fee or free service |
| Same-day delivery | Airport day or a one-way sightseeing route | Removes the need to return to the original station | Costs more and requires cutoff, destination, and collection planning | Official station-to-airport weekday examples start at KRW 20,000 |
| Airport storage counter | A layover or time spent around the terminals | Keeps the bag at the airport | Poor fit if you need the bag delivered to a Seoul hotel | Confirm current operator, terminal, hours, and quote at the airport |

Start with geography. If you will return to the same station before taking a train, a locker is efficient. If you begin in Hongdae but end at Seoul Station, a same-day transfer may save an unnecessary cross-city journey. If your hotel is close to your evening departure route, asking its front desk first can be simpler than either.

Do not compare only the listed fee. Add the subway fare, walking time, stairs, and the risk of a last-minute detour. Saving a few thousand won is not good value if collecting the bag forces you to cross Seoul during rush hour.

## T-Locker Prices, Sizes, and Hours

![Visitor using a phone beside an open subway luggage locker and carry-on](/images/seoul-luggage-storage-guide-h2-1.webp)

The official Seoul guide lists **5,557 T-Locker compartments across 336 locations in 273 stations**. That broad network is the main reason to consider a subway locker: you can choose a station that matches the day's route instead of defaulting to Seoul Station or Myeongdong.

The published locker dimensions are **width × depth × height**:

| T-Locker size | Published interior dimensions | Weekday base for 4 hours | Weekend base for 4 hours | Extra per hour |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Small | 50 × 30 × 60 cm | KRW 2,200 | KRW 3,100 | KRW 500 |
| Medium | 50 × 45 × 60 cm | KRW 3,300 | KRW 4,600 | KRW 800 |
| Large | 50 × 90 × 60 cm | KRW 4,400 | KRW 6,100 | KRW 1,000 |

Measure the bag's widest points, including wheels, handles, side pockets, and any items tied to the outside. A suitcase described by a shop as “24 inch” does not automatically fit a particular locker: product sizing conventions and actual exterior dimensions are not the same thing. Keep a photograph of your measurements, and choose a staffed alternative if the fit is close.

The official page gives T-Locker access hours as **5:00 a.m. to midnight every day** and warns that storage and retrieval are unavailable outside operating hours. Treat midnight as a hard planning boundary, not a target pickup time. Station entrances, final trains, maintenance, and unfamiliar passages can all slow you down.

For newer lockers, the city instructs users to check availability and complete payment, storage, and retrieval through the **T-Locker app**, which it identifies as Korean-language only. Older units may have different on-locker instructions. Before placing a bag inside:

1. Confirm that you are using the correct station and locker bank.
2. Photograph the nearby landmark, exit number, and locker position without capturing another customer's personal details.
3. Finish the reservation and test that the door has locked.
4. Save the retrieval method and transaction confirmation offline.
5. Set a collection alarm well before the last practical train.

Do not put your passport, wallet, medication, only phone, or flight documents in the locker. If the phone is required for retrieval, its battery and data connection are part of the plan. Carry a power bank and save any help number or confirmation you may need.

Check the booking page or official channel if a location is essential to your itinerary.

| Large | About 23–27 inches | KRW 6,000 | KRW 9,000 | KRW 1,000 per hour |

The same page gives offline counter hours as **9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. year-round** and links to online storage and delivery reservations. Confirm the specific point's availability and last acceptance time rather than assuming every service can begin at 9:59 p.m.

At handoff, look over the suitcase with staff and retain the receipt or digital confirmation. Remove loose straps and old airline tags that could catch during handling. Keep cash, valuables, electronics, travel documents, medicine, and fragile essentials in your day bag. If an item is unusually valuable or delicate, ask about exclusions and liability before payment.

## Same-Day Delivery to a Station or Incheon Airport

![Courier loading suitcases into a delivery van as travelers begin a luggage-free day in Seoul](/images/seoul-luggage-storage-guide-h2-3.webp)

Delivery becomes attractive when storage would force a return trip. T-Luggage advertises same-day movement between participating Seoul stations and between a station and Incheon Airport. The current official fee examples are:

| Bag category | Within Seoul weekday / weekend | Station–Incheon weekday / weekend |
|---|---:|---:|
| Small or medium, up to about 23 inches | KRW 17,000 / 25,000 | KRW 20,000 / 29,000 |
| Large, about 23–27 inches | KRW 26,000 / 38,000 | KRW 26,000 / 38,000 |
| Extra large, about 27–36 inches | KRW 31,000 / 45,000 | KRW 31,000 / 45,000 |

These figures are a September 5, 2026 snapshot, not a permanent quote. The airport column describes a delivery service; it is not the same as checking a suitcase onto your flight. You still need to collect the bag at the designated point, comply with your airline's check-in deadline, and carry it through the normal baggage process unless you separately qualify for an official off-airport check-in service.

Build a generous buffer. A delivery arrival that is “on time” for the service may still be too close for your airline, terminal transfer, security, or bag-drop deadline. Confirm all of the following before handing over anything:

- Exact destination counter and terminal
- Same-day acceptance cutoff
- Earliest or promised collection window
- Maximum dimensions and weight
- Items the operator will not accept
- Identification and receipt required for collection
- Delay, cancellation, and compensation terms
- Contact method that will work without a Korean phone number

Incheon Airport separately lists Hanjin Express luggage storage and delivery counters. Its official [airport service notice](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/6636/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGYmJzJTJGYXBfZW4lMkY5MzclMkYxMzgxOTUlMkZhcnRjbFZpZXcuZG8lM0Y%3D) says hours vary by terminal and location; the published examples range from 7:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. to a 24-hour Terminal 2 counter. Search the airport's current facilities directory for the operator before travel because counters and hours can change.

The airport's [Easy Drop guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1417/subview.do) describes a different product: eligible passengers on participating airlines can check in and hand over checked baggage at designated off-airport locations. Airline, route, day, check-in, and cutoff restrictions apply. Do not assume that booking ordinary luggage delivery makes you eligible for Easy Drop.

If you are traveling between the airport and Seoul Station, coordinate storage with your rail choice. ## When Hotel Luggage Storage Is the Better Choice

Ask your accommodation before paying for a separate facility. Many staffed hotels can explain whether they hold bags for registered guests before check-in or after checkout, but LoveKorea does not assume that the service is free, overnight, insured, or available to every room type.

Send a precise message:

> I have a reservation for September 12. Can you store one 26-inch suitcase from about 10:00 a.m. before check-in? Is there a fee, closing time, or item restriction?

For departure day, replace the date and ask how late collection is possible. A 24-hour front desk does not necessarily mean the luggage room accepts pickups at any hour. Guesthouses and unmanned properties may have no secure staffed area at all.

Hotel storage works best when you will naturally return to the property. It is less appealing when the hotel is in Hongdae and your final activity and airport train are near Seoul Station. Map the complete last-day route before deciding. Our [Seoul hotel neighborhood guide](/posts/seoul-hotels-top-10-myeongdong-hongdae-gangnam-guide/) can help compare the transport tradeoffs of Myeongdong, Hongdae, and Gangnam.

Never leave a bag unattended in a lobby because a message was unanswered. Get a claim tag or clear staff confirmation, and verify that the person receiving it understands when you will return. If you plan to send a courier, confirm that the property accepts third-party luggage deliveries and will match the bag to your reservation.

## Best Storage Plan by Seoul Itinerary

### Early arrival before hotel check-in

Ask the hotel first if it is on the day's route. If it is far from your first activity, choose a locker near the activity and collect the bag before heading to the hotel. Avoid storing at an interchange merely because it is famous; a smaller station beside your destination can mean less walking.

### Checkout day with an evening flight

Compare three routes: return to the hotel, store near the airport railway or bus, or arrange same-day delivery. Keep your passport and flight essentials with you. If you use AREX, identify the exact airport terminal and leave more time than the transport operator's minimum.

### Shopping in Myeongdong

A staffed T-Luggage point is listed near Myeongdong Station Exits 9 and 10, while lockers may be available elsewhere in the station network. Storage keeps shopping aisles easier to navigate, but leave space and weight allowance for purchases. Review the [Korea tourist tax-refund guide](/posts/korea-tax-refund-shopping-guide/) before packing receipts and goods into a checked suitcase.

### Day trip from Seoul Station

Use a locker or staffed counter only if the opening and collection times match both trains. Seoul Station is large, so record which concourse and level you used. A 15-minute pickup plan can fail if you return to the wrong operator's locker bank.

### Changing hotels across Seoul

Delivery may be worth the premium when it replaces two subway journeys with a full-size case. Confirm that the receiving hotel will accept the bag before you send it. Put identifying information inside the suitcase as well as on a protected external tag, but do not display unnecessary passport or home-address details.

## Luggage Safety and Common Mistakes

**Leaving the only copy of essential documents inside.** Keep passport, wallet, medication, keys, electronics, and urgent travel documents with you. A locked suitcase is not accessible when a locker app fails or a counter has closed.

**Choosing a station without planning the return.** Save the station name, line, exit, floor, operator, and locker number. Large interchanges can contain several concourses and storage systems.

**Assuming “large” means any large suitcase.** Measure the case in centimeters, including wheels. T-Locker and T-Luggage use different size systems, so their labels are not interchangeable.

**Ignoring overtime charges.** A four-hour base is not an all-day flat rate. Estimate the complete storage time and compare it with the cost of delivery or hotel storage.

**Arriving at closing.** Leave time for navigation, queues, payment problems, and support. A missed pickup can cause a missed flight or force an overnight recovery process.

**Storing prohibited, fragile, or high-value items without checking.** Read the current operator terms. If staff cannot clearly accept the item, keep it with you or find an appropriate specialist service.

**Relying on mobile data at the last second.** Download the address and confirmation, carry power, and save the support contact. Our [Korean subway and bus phrase guide](/posts/korean-subway-bus-phrases/) includes simple questions for finding exits, platforms, and help desks.

## FAQ

**Q: How much are luggage lockers in Seoul in 2026?**

As checked September 5, 2026, Seoul's official T-Locker table lists four-hour weekday bases of KRW 2,200, 3,300, and 4,400 for small, medium, and large compartments. Weekend bases are KRW 3,100, 4,600, and 6,100. Hourly overage charges apply.

**Q: Will a 28-inch suitcase fit in a Seoul subway locker?**

Do not decide from the marketed inch size alone. Compare the suitcase's complete exterior dimensions with the official locker dimensions, including wheels and handles. A staffed T-Luggage counter may be safer for an extra-large case.

**Q: Can my hotel keep my luggage after checkout?**

Possibly, but it is a property policy rather than a guaranteed Seoul-wide service. Ask your accommodation about guest eligibility, fee, security, hours, item restrictions, and the latest pickup time.

**Q: Can I send luggage from Seoul to Incheon Airport on the same day?**

T-Luggage publishes station-to-Incheon same-day delivery prices. Confirm the current origin, terminal destination, cutoff, collection window, size, and identification requirements. Build a large flight buffer and remember that ordinary delivery does not check the bag onto the aircraft.

**Q: Are Seoul subway lockers open 24 hours?**

No. The official T-Locker page lists 5:00 a.m. to midnight and says bags cannot be stored or retrieved outside operating hours. Individual station access conditions may still affect your route.

**Q: What is the difference between T-Locker and T-Luggage?**

T-Locker is a self-service subway locker network. T-Luggage is a staffed service at selected stations that offers storage and same-day delivery. Compare the bag's size, your return route, service hours, and total cost.

## Plan Your Luggage-Free Day

Measure your largest bag, mark the place where you will finish sightseeing, and work backward from the time you must collect or receive it. Then verify the live locker, counter, hotel, or delivery details. Ten minutes of route planning can turn a stressful checkout day into one final meal, museum, or neighborhood walk without a suitcase behind you.
