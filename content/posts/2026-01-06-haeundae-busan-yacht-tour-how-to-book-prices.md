**Updated September 23, 2026.** A “Haeundae yacht tour” can leave from three quite different places: Yongho Bay, Suyeong Yachting Center or a dock on the Suyeong River. Choose the route and departure point first, then compare prices for the same date and passenger count. The figures below are published prices for named products, **not a price range for every Busan yacht tour**. Check the operator's current booking page before paying.

## Which route should you book?

### Oryukdo or Haeundae from Yongho Bay

[Diamond Bay](https://diamondbay.co.kr/en/tour/) lists two 50-minute courses: Oryukdo and Haeundae/Gwangan Bridge. Its daytime route depends on conditions and cannot be selected separately, according to the operator. If Oryukdo is the reason you are booking, ask which route will run on your date before you commit. The company departs from [Yongho Bay Cruise Terminal](https://diamondbay.co.kr/en/booking/) in Nam-gu, not from Haeundae Beach.

### Suyeong River and Gwangalli from APEC Naru Park

The [Haeundae River Dinner Cruise](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000304004001000&uc_seq=2375) goes along the Suyeong River and toward Gwangalli, passing landmarks such as Busan Cinema Center, Marine City and Gwangan Bridge. The operator's [directions](https://en.haeundaerivercruise.com/ADDRESSINFO) put the ticket office at **85 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero**, near Centum City Station Exit 12. The dock is down the riverside stairs from that office. Do not navigate to Suyeong Yachting Center for this cruise.

### Public yacht from Suyeong Yachting Center

[The Yacht](https://en.theyacht.co.kr/%EC%9A%94%ED%8A%B8%ED%88%AC%EC%96%B4) sells shared tours and asks guests to confirm availability before buying. Its published address is **84 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, Room 107**. Its page does not give a price or a guaranteed route, so compare the actual departure, route, duration and total checkout price with the other options.

## Public tickets or a private charter?

A public ticket is priced per person. A private charter buys exclusive use of a vessel; its quote may depend on the boat, date, group size and extras. Compare **total public-ticket cost = ticket price × paying passengers** with the charter's **full quote**, then check whether both trips cover the route and time slot you want.

For a real private-booking example, [Diamond Bay's Midas 722 charter page](https://diamondbay.co.kr/en/private/) lists a 50-minute day charter at **₩2,200,000** and a night charter at **₩2,750,000**, including VAT. This is a large vessel with up to 88 seated passengers; those figures are **not** typical prices for a small-group yacht. Its page also advertises a group advance-booking rate “from ₩1,000,000,” which varies by group and time. Ask for a dated, all-in quote rather than budgeting from the promotional starting figure.

## Before you pay

1. **Check the exact dock.** “Haeundae” in a tour name does not mean boarding at Haeundae Beach. Use the operator's address in your confirmation.
2. **Match the route to your priority.** For Oryukdo, confirm whether that course will operate. For bridge lights, confirm the actual night route and departure time.
3. **Compare like with like.** Check adult and child counts, duration, weekday or weekend, inclusions, and any extra booking fees before comparing totals.
4. **Read the cancellation rules.** Ask how weather changes, operator cancellations and customer cancellations are handled for the specific ticket you buy.
5. **Keep the booking details handy.** Follow the operator's stated check-in time and any identification instructions in your confirmation; these requirements can differ by tour.

### Quick booking example

Two adults taking Diamond Bay's published daytime public tour would pay **₩60,000** before any discount or booking fee (2 × ₩30,000). The same operator's published night tickets would total **₩80,000** (2 × ₩40,000). These are arithmetic examples from the [operator's listed adult rates](https://diamondbay.co.kr/en/tour/), not a live checkout quote. Verify your date and route before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much is a Busan yacht tour in 2026?** Diamond Bay currently lists adult public tickets at ₩30,000 by day and ₩40,000 at night. Visit Busan lists its separate River Dinner Cruise at ₩39,000 per adult in September and October 2026. Other operators may charge differently; check the named product, date and inclusions.

**Where does a Haeundae yacht tour leave from?** There is no single boarding point. The three tours above use Yongho Bay Cruise Terminal, APEC Naru Park and Suyeong Yachting Center respectively. Follow your own booking confirmation.

**Can I choose an Oryukdo route?** Not automatically. Diamond Bay says its daytime Oryukdo/Haeundae routes cannot be selected separately and can change with weather. Confirm this before buying if Oryukdo is essential.

**Is a private tour cheaper for a group?** Calculate the full quote divided by your passenger count and compare it with public tickets for a similar route and time. Vessel size and extras change the result. Diamond Bay's published large-vessel charter price should not be treated as a small-boat market rate.
