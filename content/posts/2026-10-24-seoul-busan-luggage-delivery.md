**Same-day luggage delivery does not necessarily mean your suitcase is ready at hotel check-in.** Goodlugg currently lists **collection after 10 PM** for its Seoul–Busan hotel route, in either direction, with a **minimum of two bags**. If that timing fits your trip, keep everything you need for the first evening in a smaller bag that travels with you.

**Operator information checked October 24, 2026 (US Central time).** This is a planning comparison, not a test booking or a delivery review. Confirm your exact hotels, date and final quote before paying.

## The two-bag price check

Here is the relevant example from [Goodlugg’s official Korea delivery page](https://www.goodlugg.com/luggage-delivery/korea?language=en). All service times are Korea local time.

| Seoul hotel ↔ Busan hotel | Current displayed condition |
|---|---|
| Hand over the bags | Before 10 AM |
| Collect at the destination | After 10 PM |
| Minimum booking | Two bags |
| Checked-bag category | Up to 28 inches; length + width + height within 160 cm |
| Displayed checked-bag price | ₩48,000 per bag, one way |

**Calculation: two eligible checked bags × ₩48,000 = ₩96,000 one way.** This is the displayed base-price example; check the final booking total. Bags over 30 kg are excluded. The page requires advance booking by the preceding midnight and luggage-photo upload.

The useful comparison is the **whole cost of your proposed handoff**. If you would otherwise carry the cases to the station and onward to your hotel, decide whether avoiding those two loaded journeys is worth that quoted total to your party. Sending two bags also means packing a separate small bag for anything you cannot leave unavailable until late evening.

## Choose the handoff that fits your day

Use the table below as a decision aid, then confirm the service details for the option you prefer.

| Your plan | What to settle before booking |
|---|---|
| Check in, shower and change before dinner | Keep those clothes and toiletries with you, or obtain a confirmed earlier delivery option |
| Avoid station detours with two large cases | Check the hotel-to-hotel route and whether both properties can handle the handoff |
| Travel via the stations anyway | Compare a station-forwarding quote with carrying the cases yourself |
| Send only one case, or stay in an unattended rental | Obtain a separate suitable quote; the two-bag hotel example above does not establish eligibility |
| Stop elsewhere for one night before reaching Busan or Seoul | Agree the delivery date and receiving hotel’s storage arrangement separately |

For a one-night stop, write out the dates rather than simply asking for “same-day delivery.” For example: you leave your Seoul hotel on Monday, spend Monday night elsewhere, and check into Busan on Tuesday. Ask both the operator and the receiving hotel which day the bags would arrive, who would accept them and whether holding them until Tuesday is permitted. This is a hypothetical itinerary, not a confirmed storage arrangement.

## Zimcarry KTX forwarding is another route to price

[Zimcarry’s official KTX express page](https://www.zimcarry.net/page/zim-ktxpass.php) describes station forwarding. Its KTX express branches are **closed on Sundays**. Acceptance normally closes 30 minutes before a listed dispatch train and may close earlier if capacity fills. Use the service’s dispatch list; your own passenger train is not automatically a luggage dispatch. Collection must fit the receiving branch’s hours.

Zimcarry also links an [official courier-connection notice](https://www.zimcarry.net/bbs/board.php?bo_table=notice&wr_id=44), dated November 25, 2024, offering arranged pickup and delivery. Ask for the complete route, timing and total price. A station tariff alone is not a hotel-to-hotel quote.

A practical comparison needs the same endpoints on both sides: your **actual departing hotel** and **actual receiving hotel**. Include any trip you still have to make to a luggage counter. Check whether you must personally collect the bags and what happens if your sightseeing or train journey runs late.

If you decide to carry everything on the train, use the separate [KTX luggage allowance and storage guide](/posts/ktx-luggage-two-bag-rule/) before packing. Buying a passenger ticket and arranging luggage delivery are separate decisions.

## Get a clear answer from both hotels

Before paying, assemble one short handoff note:

- Exact hotel names and addresses, including the branch.
- Guest name used for the receiving reservation and its check-in date.
- Bag count, measurements and weights.
- Agreed handover date and time at the departing hotel.
- Expected receipt window and the receiving hotel’s staffed hours.
- Operator confirmation, tracking details and a contact for a delayed handoff.

You can send the receiving hotel this question through your reservation’s messaging channel:

> I have a reservation under [name] for [check-in date]. Can your front desk accept [number] suitcases from [delivery service] on [date], potentially after 10 PM? Will someone be available to receive and store them, and how should the bags be labeled for my reservation? Is there any handling or storage charge?

> [체크인 날짜]에 [예약자 이름]으로 예약했습니다. [날짜]에 [배송업체]에서 보내는 캐리어 [개수]개가 밤 10시 이후에 도착할 수도 있습니다. 프런트에서 수령하고 보관해 주실 수 있나요? 짐에는 예약 정보를 어떻게 표시하면 되나요? 별도 수령 또는 보관 요금이 있나요?

Goodlugg’s Korean and English cancellation wording differed when checked. **Confirm the cancellation deadline for your booking** before payment.

## Pack for the evening you actually have

Keep your passport, medication, valuables and travel documents with you. Add the charger, sleepwear, toiletries, a change of clothes and any children’s essentials you will need before the large cases arrive. Walk through your planned arrival, dinner and bedtime: if an item is needed at any of those points, it belongs in the bag you carry.

For bags that only need somewhere to wait within Seoul, compare [local luggage storage](/posts/seoul-luggage-storage-guide/). For your departure day, check the different [Incheon luggage-pickup problem before a midnight flight](/posts/incheon-luggage-delivery-midnight-flight/).

The best delivery choice is the one whose receiving window and handoff fit your itinerary. Settle those details before you remove the suitcases from your own travel plan.
