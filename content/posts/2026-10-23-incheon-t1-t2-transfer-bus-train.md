**In Incheon Airport's public area, you can change between Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 by free shuttle bus or by AREX All-Stop train for ₩1,200 per adult using a transit card.** The bus boards on **3F**; AREX is at the **B1 Transportation Center**. If you are connecting between international flights inside the secure area, check the separate transfer route before heading for either one.

The airport's [inter-terminal overview](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1525/subview.do) still displays ₩1,050. This guide uses the rail operator's [current adult transit-card fare table](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/normal/fare), which lists **₩1,200 between the two airport stations**. Official pages were checked **October 23, 2026**.

## First, identify where you are

Use your flight confirmation and the airline's current information to confirm the destination terminal. For a hotel, tour desk or pickup, check that facility's exact terminal and whether it is in the public or secure area.

| Your situation | Start here |
|---|---|
| You arrived from Seoul at the wrong terminal and have not passed departure security | Compare the public-area bus and AREX below; tell your airline if check-in time is becoming tight. |
| You have finished arrival formalities and are in the public arrivals hall | Use the public-area transport options to reach another terminal's public facilities. |
| You are arriving on one international flight and connecting to another | Confirm the transfer route and baggage arrangements with the airline before following public-arrivals signs. |
| You have already passed departure security and think you are in the wrong place | Show airport staff your boarding pass before taking a shuttle train. |

For accommodation, our [Transit Hotel versus Darakhyu guide](/posts/incheon-transit-hotel-vs-darakhyu/) explains why a room in the right terminal may still be on the wrong side of security.

## Free bus: match the direction and the door

The airport describes its inter-terminal shuttle bus as **free** in its [terminal transport explainer](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/6636/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGYmJzJTJGYXBfZW4lMkY5MzclMkYxMzgyMTAlMkZhcnRjbFZpZXcuZG8lM0Y%3D). That article is dated October 2024; use the **current route pages linked in the table** for the service details below.

| Direction | Bus and public-area pickup |
|---|---|
| **T1 → T2** | **AIRPORT 04**, T1 **3F, outside door 8**. [Current route](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/6605/subview.do) |
| **T2 → T1** | **AIRPORT 03**, T2 **3F, by door 7** in the central doors 6–7 area. [Current route](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1518/subview.do) |

These numbers identify **terminal entrance doors**, not aircraft boarding gates. At T1, the AIRPORT 03 stops by doors 3 and 12 serve the onward route toward the business and cargo areas. For **T2**, use the AIRPORT 04 pickup by door 8 instead.

The current route pages list:

- **AIRPORT 04:** 10–20-minute intervals; route operating hours 04:30–23:20.
- **AIRPORT 03:** 7–20-minute intervals; route operating hours 04:30–23:35.

Those are published service summaries, **not a promise that a bus leaves your particular stop at the final listed minute**. Near the beginning or end of service, check the stop timetable. Compare the displayed next departure before committing to the bus.

## AREX: budget the ticket you will actually use

Follow **Airport Railroad / AREX** signs to the Transportation Center on B1, as shown in the airport's [rail location guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1512/subview.do). Select the **other airport terminal** as your destination and use the All-Stop service. A Seoul-bound Express ticket is a different purchase; see our [AREX service comparison](/posts/incheon-airport-arex-guide/) if continuing into the city.

The operator's [fare table](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/normal/fare) separates the fare from the refundable deposit:

| One adult, one terminal-to-terminal trip | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Prepaid or postpaid transit card | **₩1,200** fare |
| Single-journey card | **₩1,300** fare plus **₩500** deposit |
| Cash needed for that single-journey purchase | **₩1,800**, with the ₩500 deposit refundable at the destination station |

**Example for four adults:** existing transit cards cost **4 × ₩1,200 = ₩4,800**. Four single-journey cards require **₩7,200 upfront**; after recovering all four deposits, the fares total **₩5,200**. The free bus avoids those fares. This calculation excludes buying or funding a reusable card.

## “Six minutes” is the ride, not the whole correction

The airport estimates **six minutes for the rail segment** between terminals. Add the walk to B1, ticket purchase if needed, the wait for a train and the walk from the arrival station to your check-in counter or meeting point.

Use this checklist on your phone:

1. **Deadline:** what time must you reach the airline counter, tour desk or pickup point?
2. **Current position:** are you already near B1, or near a 3F shuttle stop?
3. **Next service:** what departure is actually available now?
4. **Arrival walk:** where exactly must you go after leaving the train or bus?

Choose from the complete journey. A short train ride alone cannot establish that you will meet an airline's check-in or boarding deadline. If time is tight, contact airline staff while confirming the route.

## Connecting flights use a different shuttle train

The secure-area **IAT shuttle train** is separate from AREX. [Korean Air's international transfer guide](https://www.koreanair.com/contents/plan-your-travel/at-the-airport/incheon-airport/transfer/international-flights) requires an onward boarding pass or e-ticket itinerary to access the transfer area or IAT. Check with your airline if you need a boarding pass issued or must collect and recheck baggage.

For **T2 → T1**, the airport's [official transfer instructions](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1464/subview.do) show the route through the **Concourse**, a second shuttle train, and transfer security at T1. They also warn that **you cannot return after boarding**. Follow the signs for your onward terminal and gate; ask staff before using the train for an optional visit to another terminal.

Changing airports for a Jeju flight is a separate journey: use the [Incheon-to-Gimpo transfer guide](/posts/incheon-to-gimpo-jeju-transfer/).

## A question to show staff

For the free bus to Terminal 2:

**제2여객터미널로 가는 무료 셔틀버스 타는 곳이 어디인가요?**

“Where do I board the free shuttle bus to Terminal 2?”

For Terminal 1, change **제2여객터미널** to **제1여객터미널**. Show your booking or boarding pass as well, so staff can check the terminal and whether you need the public-area or connecting-flight route.
