# Contact

LoveKorea welcomes correction requests, editorial questions, privacy requests, and monetization inquiries.

## Best Contact Method

For now, the most reliable contact path is GitHub Issues:

https://github.com/aniposion/lovekorea.me/issues

Use this for:

- Reporting a broken image or broken link.
- Requesting a correction to a travel, price, route, or product guide.
- Reporting outdated information.
- Asking about affiliate disclosure or sponsored content.
- Requesting removal of personal information that may appear in a comment or article.

## Correction Requests

When requesting a correction, please include:

- The page URL.
- The sentence, image, or section that needs review.
- The source or reason for the correction.
- Whether the issue is urgent, such as a safety, pricing, or access problem.

We aim to keep LoveKorea practical and accurate, but travel schedules, prices, product availability, and policies can change quickly.

## Advertising and Affiliate Questions

For advertising, affiliate, or partnership questions, include:

- Your organization or website.
- The page or category you are asking about.
- Whether the request is editorial, sponsored, affiliate, or technical.

LoveKorea keeps editorial content separate from paid placements. Paid or affiliate relationships should not determine whether advice is useful to readers.

## Privacy Requests

For privacy-related questions, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) and include the page URL or comment thread involved in your request.

## Please Do Not Send

Please do not send confidential documents, passwords, private account details, payment card information, or sensitive personal data through public issue threads.
