LoveKorea publishes English-language guides to traveling in South Korea and exploring Korean food, culture, shopping, and everyday life.

Our aim is to help readers make practical decisions: how to get from an airport to their destination, what to check before booking an activity, and how to understand local customs and products.

## What You Will Find

- [Korea travel guides](/k-travel/) covering destinations, transport, activities, and trip planning.
- [Korean food guides](/k-food/) with recipes, ingredients, and dining information.
- [Korean beauty guides](/k-beauty/) about products and shopping.
- [Korean language guides](/learn-korean/) for useful phrases and cultural context.

## How Content Is Produced

LoveKorea uses AI-assisted research, drafting, and illustration as part of its publishing process. Articles can contain errors, and schedules, prices, availability, and travel rules can change after publication. Check the sources linked in a guide and confirm details with the relevant operator or official website before making a booking or traveling.

The site includes AI-generated illustrations. These images illustrate an article's subject and should not be treated as evidence of a visit, product test, or the current appearance of a particular place.

If a detail is wrong or out of date, please send the page address and a supporting source to [admin@yoonisoft.com](mailto:admin@yoonisoft.com). This also provides a way to ask about the sources or images used in a guide.
