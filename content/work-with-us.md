LoveKorea publishes practical English guides for people planning a trip to Korea. We welcome project inquiries from accommodation providers, tour operators, travel services and brands that can help those readers.

LoveKorea is operated by **Yoonisoft LLC**. Project scope, fees, delivery dates and usage rights are agreed in writing before work begins.

## English travel content for your business

Commission a clear, useful English guide or customer information page for your own website. A project can cover an arrival guide, a destination overview, a product comparison or an FAQ that explains what guests need to know before booking.

A typical brief includes:

- One defined topic, audience and deliverable.
- Research against your current official product information and relevant primary sources.
- Clear explanations of inclusions, exclusions, access and booking steps.
- A draft for factual review and one agreed revision round.

Send your website, topic, intended audience, deadline and budget to [request a content quote](mailto:admin@yoonisoft.com?subject=LoveKorea%20content%20project).

## Sponsored guides and placements

We consider paid features and placements when the subject fits an existing reader need. A proposal should explain the product, the useful information it adds and the page or topic you have in mind.

Sponsored content is clearly labeled. We retain editorial review, identify material limitations and use appropriate sponsored-link attributes. Search rankings, bookings and audience numbers are not guaranteed. A sponsorship does not buy a positive review or a claim of personal experience.

Email [a sponsorship brief](mailto:admin@yoonisoft.com?subject=LoveKorea%20sponsorship%20inquiry) with your business website, proposed topic, campaign dates, budget and any requested deliverables. We can discuss the relevant page's measured performance before agreeing to a placement.

## Affiliate and referral partnerships

We consider relevant travel products with clear prices, customer support, cancellation terms and reliable referral reporting. Please include your official program page, eligible markets, commission basis, attribution rules and payout conditions.

[Send an affiliate partnership proposal](mailto:admin@yoonisoft.com?subject=LoveKorea%20affiliate%20partnership).

1. Email **admin@yoonisoft.com** with your brief and business website.
